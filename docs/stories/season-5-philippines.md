# Season 5: "Kwento Nila", personal horror experiences shared online (Episodes 41–50)

**The brief (user, 2026-11-15):** local Philippine personal experiences that people have shared on public platforms, retold with the new PSX models, "more spooky more scary and more horror". On rights, the user decided: "we dont have to adapt their real identity just their personal story … we dont need permission people already shared it through public platforms."

**How we retell (our safeguards):**
- **Our own words:** we take the experience (what happened), never the poster's text. No quoted sentences.
- **No identity:** no names, usernames, photos, exact addresses, employers, schools or hospitals; the narrator is a composite "ako".
- **Changed details:** family names, the exact building, the barangay, the year.
- **Framing:** each post says "batay sa mga karanasang ibinahagi online" (based on experiences shared online), and nothing is presented as verified.
- **Takedown:** if a poster recognises their story and objects, the user decides whether to take the video down or credit them.
- **Not used:** stories tied to a real crime or a real victim (for example, the school CR ghost said to be an assault victim).

**Research limits:** Reddit, Facebook, TikTok and X can't be opened from this environment. `cebuinsights.com` and `yourghoststories.com` were refused by the session's network policy (they'd have to be allowed in the environment's network settings). Everything below is from search summaries of public posts, forum threads, blogs and press roundups that quote people's own experiences. The source column is for our records only and is never shown in a video or post.

**Season standards:**
- **Picture:** the PSX models (`src/horror/psx/models.tsx`), posed by aim.
- **Scarier rig animation:** every episode has at least one wrong movement, for example:
  - a head turning past human limits;
  - a stop-motion step (the pose held, then snapped);
  - a crawl or a walk backwards;
  - a figure closer with every cut;
  - a too-still body that breathes once.
- **Faces:** mostly in shadow, with longer holds before the reveal.
- **Kept from Season 4:** Taglish with Juanito, the eerie score and the seamless loop.
- **Kept from earlier seasons:** the motion rule, hard cuts, loudness, care.
