# Season 1: ten 70-second horror stories

Date: 2026-11-10. Channel direction from the user: horror storytelling (real-life cases, viral scary stories and our own originals) told in **pixel 3D analog horror**, starting with shorts and building towards long-form episodes. *What if* science videos are retired.

Each script runs **about 68–79 seconds**: 144–172 spoken words at a slow narration pace (about 145 words per minute plus the tagged pauses). Scripts use the Google Vids audio-tag format (`[slow pace]`, `[suspenseful]`, `[short pause]`, `[medium pause]` and so on). For ElevenLabs, delete the tags and keep the line breaks. Every script is under the 2,500-character Vids scene limit.

## 1. The Mask on Channel 11 (true)

**Hook frame:** a TV in a dark room cuts to black, then a masked face sways in front of a spinning metal sheet.
**Shots:** Chicago skyline at night → living-room CRT with the sports news → hard cut to the mask (low-res, rolling sync bars) → transmitter tower → second TV, sci-fi show → mask returns with distorted audio → the flyswatter, kept off-screen with only the sound and the mask's reaction → empty static.

```
[suspenseful] [slow pace] On November 22nd, 1987, Chicago was watching the evening sports news.
[short pause] Then the screen went black.
[short pause] A man in a rubber mask appeared, swaying in front of a spinning sheet of metal. No voice. Just a buzz.
[medium pause] Engineers switched the signal. Twenty-five seconds later, he was gone.
[medium pause] Two hours later, he came back.
[short pause] This time on a different channel, in the middle of a science-fiction show.
[short pause] And this time, he spoke.
His voice was warped. He laughed. He moaned. He hummed a cartoon theme song and held up a can of soda.
[short pause] For ninety seconds, nobody at the station could stop him.
[medium pause] The last thing viewers saw was the man bent over, and someone off-screen hitting him with a flyswatter.
[medium pause] Then static.
[medium pause] Federal investigators searched for him. Rumours named suspects. No one was ever charged.
[short pause] More than thirty-five years later, nobody knows who was under the mask.
[medium pause] [thoughtful] Or how easily he got inside the television in your living room.
```

**Facts:** the Max Headroom signal hijacking, 22 Nov 1987. Around 9:14 pm a roughly 25-second silent intrusion on WGN-TV's sports report. Around 11:15 pm a roughly 90-second intrusion with audio on WTTW during *Doctor Who*. Investigated by the FCC; never solved. Don't name any suspect, and avoid the word "never caught" in titles in a way that implies an accusation.

## 2. This Is the Voice of Vrillon (true)

**Hook frame:** a 1970s newsreader's mouth keeps moving while a different voice speaks.
**Shots:** English town, winter dusk → family living room, wood-panel TV → newsreader shot, audio dissolves into a hum → slow push into the screen as the voice speaks → transmitter mast on a hill with a blinking light → back to normal news, the family frozen.

```
[slow pace] [suspenseful] On November 26th, 1977, families in southern England sat down to watch the early evening news.
[short pause] Then the newsreader's voice dissolved into a low, buzzing hum.
[short pause] And another voice came through.
[medium pause] Calm. Distorted. Speaking directly to them.
[short pause] [whisper] "This is the voice of Vrillon, a representative of the Ashtar Galactic Command, speaking to you."
[medium pause] For about six minutes, the picture never changed. The newsreader kept moving his lips.
[short pause] But the words coming out of the television belonged to someone else.
[medium pause] The voice warned humanity to destroy its weapons of evil. It said there was only a short time left.
[short pause] Then the news came back, as if nothing had happened.
[medium pause] The station apologised. Engineers found that someone had broken into the sound feed at a single transmitter.
[short pause] Whoever did it was never identified.
[medium pause] Almost everyone agrees it was a hoax.
[short pause] [thoughtful] But for six minutes, an entire region heard their television say it was not speaking from Earth.
```

**Facts:** the Southern Television broadcast interruption, Saturday 26 Nov 1977, around 5:10 pm, through the Hannington transmitter. The audio was replaced for about six minutes while the ITN picture continued. The perpetrator was never identified. Keep the quoted line close to the widely reported wording, and paraphrase the rest.

## 3. The Lighthouse That Went Dark (true)

**Hook frame:** a lighthouse on a black island with its lamp unlit, and waves exploding below.
**Shots:** passing steamer at night, the dark tower → storm-blocked relief boat → landing with nobody waiting, flagpole bare → interior: kitchen clock stopped, a single oilskin on a hook → west landing, bent iron railings far above the sea → slate log → doorway POV into the storm → empty sea at dawn.

```
[slow pace] [suspenseful] In December 1900, a ship passing a tiny island off the coast of Scotland noticed something wrong.
[short pause] The lighthouse was dark.
[medium pause] Storms kept the relief boat away until the day after Christmas.
When it finally landed, no one came down to meet it. No flag. No keepers.
[short pause] Inside, the clock in the kitchen had stopped.
One set of oilskin coats still hung on its hook.
[medium pause] Three men had lived on that island. All three were gone.
[medium pause] At the western landing, the storm had ripped iron railings out of the rock, high above the waterline.
[short pause] The last entries on the keepers' slate were dated December 15th.
[medium pause] Investigators believed two of the men went down to secure equipment during the storm.
The third ran out without his coat, maybe to call them back.
[short pause] And a single enormous wave took them all.
[medium pause] Their bodies were never found.
[medium pause] [thoughtful] And no one will ever know what that last man saw from the doorway... that made him run out into the storm.
```

**Facts:** Flannan Isles lighthouse. The steamer *Archtor* reported the light dark on 15 Dec 1900, and the relief vessel *Hesperus* arrived on 26 Dec. Missing were James Ducat, Thomas Marshall and Donald MacArthur. Superintendent Robert Muirhead's report proposed the freak-wave explanation. **Do not use** the famous dramatic "logbook entries" (storm, praying, crying): they are a later fabrication. Likewise avoid the "overturned chair" and "uneaten meal" details unless they can be sourced.

## 4. Nine Hikers, One Cut Tent (true)

**Hook frame:** a tent slashed open from the inside, flapping on a snow slope under moonlight.
**Shots:** nine torches climbing a ridge → tent pitched on the slope at night → torn canvas from inside → footprints in socks and bare feet leading downhill → cedar tree and a dead campfire → snow ravine → Soviet file stamp → avalanche model diagram rendered as a slow slab crack → the tent alone.

```
[slow pace] [suspenseful] In February 1959, nine experienced hikers pitched their tent on a mountain slope in the northern Urals.
[short pause] Weeks later, searchers found that tent.
It had been cut open... from the inside.
[medium pause] Footprints led away down the slope. Socks. Bare feet. In temperatures far below freezing.
[short pause] They had walked more than a kilometre into the dark... and none of them came back.
[medium pause] Over the next three months, all nine were found.
Some had frozen beside the remains of a small fire under a cedar tree.
Others lay in a ravine, with injuries a doctor compared to a car crash.
[medium pause] The Soviet file closed with a single phrase: a "compelling natural force."
[short pause] For sixty years, people blamed weapons tests, secret police, even lights in the sky.
[medium pause] In 2021, scientists showed how a small, delayed slab avalanche could explain the tent and the injuries.
[short pause] Not everyone is convinced.
[medium pause] [thoughtful] Because whatever happened that night... nine people decided the frozen dark was safer than staying inside.
```

**Facts:** the Dyatlov Pass incident, night of 1–2 Feb 1959, on Kholat Syakhl. The tent was found on 26 Feb; the last bodies were found in May. The phrase "compelling natural force" comes from the 1959 case closure. The slab-avalanche model is Gaume & Puzrin, *Communications Earth & Environment* (2021), and the Russian prosecutors' 2020 review also concluded avalanche. These were real victims: no gore, and none of the lurid details.

## 5. The Night the Lake Breathed Out (true)

**Hook frame:** a still blue crater lake at night, then white mist rising from its centre.
**Shots:** village huts and cattle under stars → rumble, the lake surface bulging → mist spilling over the rim and flowing down valleys like a liquid → silent village at dawn, nobody moving (show empty paths, never bodies) → lake now rust-red → cross-section of the gas layer under water pressure → modern degassing pipe with a fountain → another lake, perfectly calm.

```
[slow pace] [suspenseful] On the night of August 21st, 1986, villagers near Lake Nyos in Cameroon heard a deep rumble.
[short pause] Some saw a white mist rise from the water.
[medium pause] By morning, the valley was silent.
[short pause] One thousand, seven hundred and forty-six people had died, in their homes, in their beds, beside the road.
Cattle lay in the fields. Even the insects were gone.
[medium pause] Survivors described a strange smell... and then nothing at all.
[medium pause] The killer was invisible.
[short pause] Deep beneath the lake, volcanic carbon dioxide had been building up for years, held down by the weight of the water above it.
That night, something disturbed it.
[short pause] The lake erupted like a shaken bottle. A cloud of gas, heavier than air, poured down the valleys and pushed the oxygen away.
[medium pause] The next morning, the blue lake had turned red.
[short pause] Today, pipes vent the gas from the bottom so it cannot build up again.
[medium pause] [thoughtful] But scientists are watching other lakes on Earth... that are still holding their breath.
```

**Facts:** the Lake Nyos limnic eruption, 21 Aug 1986, with 1,746 deaths and about 3,500 livestock killed. Degassing pipes have operated since 2001. The other lakes being watched include Lake Kivu (and, earlier, Lake Monoun in 1984). This is a recent disaster with living survivors: be respectful, show no bodies, and treat it as science rather than spectacle.

## 6. Footprints in the Snow (true)

**Hook frame:** fresh footprints in snow running from a dark forest to a farmhouse door, and none leading away.
**Shots:** isolated farm at dusk → footprint trail → hand holding an unfamiliar newspaper → empty key hook → ceiling at night, creaking footsteps overhead, dust falling → lantern in the attic finding nothing → farm at night with one window lit → days later, smoke from the chimney while the farm is silent → cattle fed in the barn → the attic hatch, open.

```
[slow pace] [suspenseful] In March 1922, a farmer in Bavaria found footprints in the fresh snow outside his isolated farm.
[short pause] They came out of the forest, all the way to the house.
[short pause] None led back out.
[medium pause] Over the next few days, small things went wrong.
A set of house keys disappeared. A newspaper turned up that nobody in the house had bought.
At night, the farmer heard footsteps in the attic.
[short pause] He searched with a lantern. He found nothing. And when neighbours offered help, he turned them down.
[medium pause] Months earlier, the family's maid had quit... saying the house was haunted.
[medium pause] On the night of March 31st, all six people at the farm called Hinterkaifeck were killed.
[short pause] When neighbours finally came, days later, someone had been feeding the cattle.
[short pause] Smoke had been seen rising from the chimney.
[medium pause] The killer had stayed in the house.
[medium pause] The case was never solved.
[short pause] [thoughtful] And no one knows how long the stranger had been living above them... before that night.
```

**Facts:** the Hinterkaifeck murders, Bavaria, night of 31 Mar–1 Apr 1922; discovered on 4 Apr. The footprints, missing keys, unfamiliar newspaper and attic footsteps come from neighbours' accounts in the police files. The previous maid had left about six months earlier. Unsolved; there was a cold-case review in 2007. Six real victims, including children: no violence on screen, only the house and its traces.

## 7. Rules for the Night Shift (original)

**Hook frame:** a laminated rule sheet taped beside a weather-station monitor, snow hammering the window.
**Shots:** cable car arriving at a mountain station → rule sheet, each rule appearing as it is read → roof anemometer spinning on a windless night → wall phone with its cord cut → window and smooth snow, then footprints → knocking at the door; peephole shows only snow → clock at 4:00 → temperature log, every row already filled in → slow turn towards the dark behind the chair. Cut before anything is visible.

```
[slow pace] [thoughtful] Welcome to Kestrel Ridge Weather Station. You'll be alone up here until the morning shift. Please follow these rules.
[short pause] One. Record the temperature every hour, on the hour.
[short pause] Two. The wind sensor on the roof sometimes reports wind on calm nights. This is a fault. Do not go up to check it.
[short pause] Three. If the wall phone rings, let it ring. This building has no phone line.
[medium pause] Four. The snow outside the window should be smooth. If you see footprints leading towards the door, turn off every light, and stay away from the glass until the snow fills them in.
[short pause] Five. You may hear knocking. Knocking is fine.
[medium pause] Six. At four a.m., someone will knock and say they are the morning shift.
The morning shift arrives at seven.
[short pause] Do not open the door. Not even if you recognise the voice.
[medium pause] [suspenseful] Especially if you recognise the voice.
[medium pause] Seven. If you don't remember writing any of the entries in tonight's temperature log...
[short pause] then you were not the one who wrote them.
[medium pause] [whisper] Don't turn around.
```

## 9. Room Zero (original)

**Hook frame:** a thermal-camera view of a cold grey hallway with one warm, body-temperature rectangle behind a blank wall.
**Shots:** inspector's van at an empty house → frosted rooms, breath visible → thermal-camera interface (pixel heat palette) → blank hallway wall → warm rectangle → tape measure outside, the two-metre gap → knuckles knock; two answering knocks, felt as wall vibration → report screen "possible water damage" → weeks later, a phone photo from the buyers: the same wall, and a door.

```
[thoughtful] I inspect houses for a living. I carry a thermal camera, because heat shows you what walls are hiding. Leaks. Wiring. Mice.
[short pause] Last month I inspected a two-storey house that had been empty for a year.
Power off. Heating off. Cold as a fridge.
[medium pause] Upstairs, at the end of the hallway, there's a blank wall. On the floor plan, there's nothing behind it.
[short pause] [suspenseful] On my camera, there was a warm rectangle. Room-sized. About body temperature.
[medium pause] I measured the house from the outside. There was a gap. About two metres of house you can't reach from inside.
[medium pause] I knocked on the wall.
[short pause] Something knocked back. Twice.
[medium pause] I left. I wrote it up as "possible water damage." I didn't tell the buyers.
[short pause] They moved in last Friday.
[medium pause] This morning, they sent me a photo and a question.
[short pause] They want to know why the wall at the end of the hallway is warm to the touch.
[medium pause] [whisper] And why there's a door in it now.
```

## 10. The Sound That Matched Us (original)

**Hook frame:** a sonar waterfall display with a second, identical trace slowly appearing behind the boat.
**Shots:** submarine diving in black water → cramped sonar room, red light → headphones, waterfall display → contact bearing astern → side-by-side signatures matching perfectly → "all stop": the crew frozen, condensation dripping → three knocks heard through the hull, everyone looking up → the cook standing beside the operator → surfacing at dawn → walking the deck aft: a long scrape down the stern, from outside.

```
[slow pace] On my second patrol, I was the sonar operator on a submarine three hundred metres down.
[short pause] My job was to listen.
[medium pause] On day nine, I picked up a contact. Slow. Steady. Directly behind us.
[short pause] I ran it through the library. No match. So I compared it to the only thing left.
[short pause] [suspenseful] Us.
[medium pause] Same propeller. Same number of blades. Same tiny rattle from a loose panel we'd been meaning to fix since port.
[medium pause] It was our sound. Coming from behind us.
[short pause] The captain ordered all stop. We drifted in silence for an hour.
[short pause] The contact stopped too.
[medium pause] Then, through the hull, every one of us heard it. Not on sonar. Not through headphones.
[short pause] A slow knock. Three times. Exactly the way our cook knocks on the galley hatch.
[medium pause] Our cook was standing right next to me.
[medium pause] [thoughtful] When we surfaced, there was nothing behind us. Only a long scrape down the stern...
[short pause] [whisper] from the outside.
```

## Using other people's stories (Reddit, creepypasta)

Stories posted on Reddit (r/nosleep, r/LetsNotMeet, r/Glitch_in_the_Matrix and similar) belong to their authors. r/nosleep's rules say narrators and channels need the author's permission. Many horror channels have had videos struck or demonetised over this. The safe paths:

1. **Ask the author** by Reddit DM. Many say yes for credit and a link. Keep the screenshot of their permission with the episode files, and credit them in the description.
2. **Documented real events** (like stories 1–6): facts can't be copyrighted. Write our own telling, check the details against reliable sources, and don't reuse another channel's script.
3. **Originals** (like 7–10): fully ours, and the best long-term asset, since they can grow into recurring places and series.

## Toward episodes and long-form

- **Shorts (60–75 s)** test which hooks and formats work.
- **Long-form episodes (10–20 min, 16:9)**:
  - *Case files*: one true case told in depth (Flannan Isles or Hinterkaifeck would carry 12–15 minutes).
  - *Recovered tapes*: three or four connected originals per episode.
  - *Compilations*: the season's shorts, re-cut and extended.
- **A connecting thread for the originals.** Kestrel Ridge, the emergency-alert network and the submarine can all be "recordings" collected by one unseen archivist. That gives the channel a recurring identity and a reason to binge.
- Optionally rename the channel, since *Odd Atom* says science. Odd Atom could stay as an "odd" brand, or a new horror-specific name could launch alongside it. Pick before the first upload in the new direction, because changing it later loses momentum.

All true-story facts above were written from well-established public accounts. Re-check dates and figures against a reliable source before publishing, especially anything you change.
