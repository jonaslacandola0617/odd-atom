# Season 6: "Shared at Night", scary personal experiences from around the world (Episodes 51–60)

**Draft, 2026-11-16. Not approved yet.** Production waits for the user.

**The brief:** `docs/stories/season-6-brief.md`. The user asked for English narration, personal experiences shared online from anywhere in the world, some gore, every episode unique, and animation that is "100" scary.

**How we retell (the Season 5 safeguards, carried over):**
- **Our own words:** we tell what happened, never the poster's text. Nothing is quoted.
- **No identity:** no names, usernames, photos, exact addresses, employers, schools or hospitals. The narrator is a composite "I".
- **Changed details:** names, the exact building, the town and the year (marked per episode below).
- **Framing:** every post says "based on experiences shared online" and claims nothing as verified.
- **Takedown:** if a poster objects, the user decides whether to take the video down or credit them.
- **Gore:** only on the entity, on animals, or on the dead in a story with no real victim. Never on a real person, never tied to a real crime, never on a minor.

**Research limits (this session):**
- Reddit, X and TikTok can't be opened from here.
- These were refused by the network policy: `goodreads.com` (the North Carolina mimic account), `legit.ng` (the Nigerian mortuary post) and `diariodecuyo.com.ar` (an Argentine Twitter thread of ghost experiences). They'd have to be allowed in the environment's network settings.
- Everything below is from search summaries of the posts and of press or wiki write-ups about them. Where a detail comes only from later retellings, the slate says so.
- Sources are for our records only and are never shown in a video or post.

## Care, per episode

- **51:** the grandparents are kind and save him. No real village, and the shrine is not named.
- **52:** lechuza belief is Mexican and Mexican-American folklore, told with respect. No neighbour is accused of being a witch, and no utility company is named.
- **53:** the dead are treated with respect. No real hospital. The mortuary scandals in the news (organ theft, neglect) are left out. No religion is mocked.
- **54:** the babies are safe in every frame, and nothing happens to them.
- **55:** churel belief is tied to women who died badly; she is told as folklore, never as a joke, and no real road or shrine is named as hers.
- **56:** Javanese belief is treated with respect: the students broke the rule, not the village. No village is named. The thread's claimed deaths are not stated; the friend's fate is left open.
- **57:** the poster isn't named, and nothing claims she died. The last post is ours.
- **58:** the children are the entity, never hurt. No real estate or street.
- **59:** the poster is a public figure, so every detail is changed (the city, the pets, the flat). No name, no handle, and no material from the 2023 film. The child's injury from the original story is never shown.
- **60:** in our retelling the daughter is an adult (a changed detail; in the thread she's a teenager), so no minor is shown possessed or hurt. The gore is on an animal. Healers and monks are shown with respect. The film's designs and its specific scenes are not used.
