# TRANSFERS — Episode 1: "Bayad Na" (Paid in Full)

> **Draft v1 (2026-11-12). Not approved.** Written to the series bible
> (`BIBLE.md`). Target running time **5:20**.

## How to read this script

- **Frames:**
  - `[2.39]` marks a real scene, letterboxed.
  - `[4:3 TAPE]` marks VHS playback, full height, with a date stamp and VHS damage.
  - `[16:9 PHONE]` marks a phone recording at full frame, with no bars.
- **Dialogue:** each line is in Taglish, followed by its English caption (*EN:*).
  Tape dialogue is mono and hissy; its captions are in italics and brackets.
- **Sound cues** are tagged by layer: **AMB** (ambience), **FOL** (foley) and
  **HOR** (horror). A file name such as `vhs-insert` is a sound already in
  library v1 (`public/assets/audio/transfers/sfx-v1/`), rotated across its four
  takes. Anything marked **NEW** still has to be generated.
- **Expressions** use the cast's painted sets (README / CHATGPT-PROMPTS): neutral,
  focused, forced smile, worried, frozen fear and so on.

**Cast:**
- On screen: NICO and BEA.
- On the phone: LIZA.
- On the tape: TITO BOY (off screen, behind the camera), TATAY RUDY, LOLA and
  JOJO (8), with three or four guests from behind.

**Locations:**
1. The shop.
2. The tape's living room.

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:44)

**[4:3 TAPE] INT. A SMALL LIVING ROOM, 1991 — NIGHT**

Black. Tape hiss comes in before the picture does. Tracking lines roll up and
settle.

Date stamp, lower right: **JUN.14 1991 8:12PM**

A despedida in a small concrete-and-plywood living room. A fluorescent tube.
Monobloc chairs. A long table under a plastic floral tablecloth: pancit, a cake,
bottles of softdrinks. A hand-painted banner on the wall: **INGAT, TATAY RUDY!**
To frame left, the kitchen doorway is a black rectangle.

The camera is handheld, cheerful and a little drunk. It swings across guests seen
from behind to TATAY RUDY (40s) at the head of the table. He wears a brand-new
polo shirt with the price tag still on the sleeve.

> **AMB:** party chatter (NEW: "Filipino family party in a small room, 1990s,
> heard through a camcorder mic"), an original upbeat song on a cassette player,
> a stand fan.
> **FOL:** camcorder handling (`camcorder-room`).

**TITO BOY** (O.S., behind the camera, a salesman's warmth)
O, kaway kayo! Para kay Tatay Rudy 'to!
*EN: [Come on, wave! This one's for Tatay Rudy!]*

Guests wave. Rudy covers his face, laughing.

LOLA (70s) comes into frame and presses a **blue airmail envelope with a
red-and-blue border** into Rudy's hand.

**LOLA**
Pambaon mo, anak. Huwag mong gastusin agad.
*EN: [Something for your trip, son. Don't spend it all at once.]*

**RUDY**
'Nay naman.
*EN: [Oh, Ma.]*

He tucks the envelope into his shirt pocket.

**TITO BOY** (O.S., lightly, buried under the chatter)
Pare, ikaw naman.
*EN: [Your turn, pare.]*

The image whips across the ceiling tube and hands fumble: **the hand-over**. The
camera is now held lower and less steadily, close to a laughing mouth.

> **FOL:** a hard handling bump, and a hand rubbing the mic.

**RUDY** (now behind the camera, close to the mic)
Paano 'to? Ano'ng pipindutin ko?
*EN: [How does this work? What do I press?]*

**TITO BOY** (O.S., farther away)
Wala. Huwag mo lang patayin.
*EN: [Nothing. Just don't turn it off.]*

Rudy films his family clumsily: a long, loving hold on Lola, then on his son JOJO
(8), who holds a paper plate of spaghetti and grins into the lens.

**RUDY**
Dito na lang. Picture tayo, lahat!
*EN: [I'll leave it here. Photo, everybody!]*

The frame tilts and drops. **The camera is set down** on the TV cabinet, low and
slightly crooked. Now the whole room is in frame: the family gathering in the
centre, Rudy hurrying into the group, and the black kitchen doorway at frame left.

> **FOL:** `camcorder-setdown`.

Someone with a still camera counts down.

**GUEST** (O.S.)
Isa, dalawa…
*EN: [One, two…]*

**FLASH.** The tape blooms white for two frames, then recovers. Everyone laughs.

*(Plant, for rewatch only: in the flash frames, the kitchen doorway holds a tall
vertical shape just darker than the dark. It is invisible at normal speed.)*

> **AMB:** the chatter continues.

The tape drops out and cuts to black. **All sound cuts with it.**

**TITLE:** *TRANSFERS*, small, tape-white, on black. Two seconds of silence.

## SCENE 1 (0:44–1:52)

**[2.39] INT. NICO'S VIDEO TRANSFER — EARLY EVENING**

Rain on the GI-sheet roof. The shutter is up. Out on the wet street, a tricycle
passes; the market across the road is closing.

NICO (34) works at the counter. He is labelling a USB stick for a customer while a
stranger's wedding plays silently on the CRT. His glasses catch the screen.

BEA (9), in her school uniform, does homework on the bench at the side of the shop,
beside **the dark back doorway** to the storeroom. Her backpack sits on the bench.

Nico's phone lies on the counter, on speaker.

> **AMB:** `rain-tin` (loop), `fan` (oscillating), the street (NEW: "provincial
> Philippine market street at dusk in light rain, tricycle passing").
> **FOL:** a marker on a USB label, and the cassette clack of a tape being cased
> (`cassette`).

**LIZA** (V.O., on the phone, in a hospital locker room, a PA announcement faint
behind her)
Nico, tumawag ulit 'yung bangko. Tatlong buwan na tayong late.
*EN: Nico, the bank called again. We're three months behind.*

**NICO**
Alam ko. May papasok na pera. Promise.
*EN: I know. Money's coming in. I promise.*

**LIZA** (V.O.)
Galing saan? Diyan sa channel mo?
*EN: From where? That channel of yours?*

Nico glances at the capture PC. A browser tab shows a plain channel dashboard,
**LUMANG TAPE**, with **Kita ngayong buwan: ₱1,840** ("Earnings this month").

**NICO**
Kumikita na siya, Liz.
*EN: It's making money now, Liz.*

**LIZA** (V.O.)
Isang libo't walong daan. Sa isang buwan. Nico… ibenta na natin 'yung shop.
Please.
*EN: Eighteen hundred. In a month. Nico… let's sell the shop. Please.*

A beat. Nico rubs his eyes under his glasses.

**NICO**
Isang buwan pa.
*EN: One more month.*

**LIZA** (V.O., tired, letting it go)
Pakainin mo si Bea. Bea, huwag mong hayaang magpuyat si Papa, ha?
*EN: Feed Bea. Bea, don't let Papa stay up late, okay?*

**BEA** (not looking up)
Opo, Ma.
*EN: Yes, Ma.*

The call ends. Bea looks at the PC screen. Nico **minimises the dashboard** before
she can read it.

**BEA**
Pa, ano po 'yung "Lumang Tape"?
*EN: Pa, what's "Old Tape"?*

**NICO**
Wala. Trabaho.
*EN: Nothing. Work.*

Bea looks past him, out at the street.

**BEA**
Pa, may box po sa labas. Nababasa na.
*EN: Pa, there's a box outside. It's getting wet.*

Nico ducks out to the step and comes back with a soaked cardboard box. A waybill in
a plastic sleeve is beaded with rain. CLOSE on the waybill:

> **PREPAID — 9 TAPES — FOR TRANSFER**
> **TO: NICO'S VIDEO TRANSFER**
> **FROM:** *(blank)*

**NICO**
Walang sender.
*EN: No sender.*

> **FOL:** tape tearing off cardboard, and a wet box set down on the counter (NEW).

He cuts it open. Nine black VHS tapes stand in a row, with no labels and no cases.
On top lies **the same blue airmail envelope**, now soft with age. Inside are
banknotes and a card in block capitals:

> **PAKIKOPYA LAHAT. BAYAD NA.**
> *EN: PLEASE COPY THEM ALL. IT'S PAID.*

He counts quickly and professionally: ₱9,000. He breathes out. It is the first time
he has looked relieved.

**BEA** (at his elbow, looking at the notes)
Pa, iba po 'yung itsura ng pera.
*EN: Pa, the money looks different.*

**NICO** (barely looking)
Luma lang. Tatanggapin pa 'yan sa bangko.
*EN: It's just old. The bank will still take it.*

He pockets it. Then, softly, a finger to his lips:

**NICO**
Shh. Huwag mo munang sasabihin kay Mama, ha?
*EN: Shh. Don't tell Mama yet, okay?*

Bea looks at him a moment too long.

**BEA**
…Opo.
*EN: …Okay.*

## SCENE 2 (1:52–3:38)

**[2.39] INT. SHOP — NIGHT**

The shutter is pulled halfway down and the street is dark beyond it. The rain is
steady. Only the desk lamp and the CRT are on.

Bea lies on the bench under Nico's jacket. Her head is near the back doorway and her
eyes are half open.

Nico lifts Tape 1 out of the box with both hands and blows dust off the flap.

> **FOL:** `cassette`, then `vhs-insert`, then `vcr-play`, then `crt-on` (with its
> thin whine, which stays in the mix).
> **AMB:** rain, the fan, and the CRT whine.

At the PC he clicks RECORD. CLOSE on the capture window: **CAPTURE_001**, its
progress bar starting to fill.

*(Plant: from this moment the shop is part of the chain.)*

**[4:3 TAPE]** The despedida again: the hand-over, the set-down, the flash. Picture
search lines tear across the frame as Nico fast-forwards past what we have seen,
then he lets it play.

**[4:3 TAPE] INT. THE LIVING ROOM, 1991 — NIGHT**

Date stamp: **JUN.14 1991 8:31PM**

**GUEST** (O.S.)
Sa labas tayo! Kasama 'yung jeep!
*EN: [Outside, everyone! With the jeep!]*

The family files out through the front door, laughing. Then the room is empty: the
fan turning, the banner stirring in its breeze, the table of food, and the kitchen
doorway, black.

> **AMB (tape):** chatter going outside, muffled; the fan in the tape; hiss.

**[2.39]** Nico watches, bored. His finger moves to the FF button.

**[4:3 TAPE]** **The picture breathes.** It goes soft, then sharp, then soft: the
camcorder's autofocus is hunting, as if something in the room has drawn it.

> **HOR:** **the focus hunt**, the series' entity signature (NEW: "camcorder
> autofocus motor hunting, tiny servo whirring back and forth, close-mic through
> the camera's own microphone", four takes). Under it, very low, `tape-breath`.

**[2.39]** Nico's finger stops over the button.

**BEA** (behind him, quiet)
Pa.
*EN: Pa.*

**NICO**
Hm?
*EN: Hm?*

**BEA**
Sino po 'yung nakatayo sa may kusina?
*EN: Who's that standing by the kitchen?*

Nico looks at her, then back at the screen. His expression goes from neutral to
focused.

**[4:3 TAPE]** A slow push in on the kitchen doorway (a digital zoom on the tape,
so the grain grows). In the black of the doorway stands a **tall figure,
perfectly still, facing the lens.** Its arms hang too long. Its face is a smear,
like a paused frame. The tracking noise crawls over the picture, and the figure
is so still that the noise moves while it doesn't.

> **AMB:** the fan in the shop **drops out of the mix**. Rain and the CRT whine
> remain.

**NICO** (quiet)
…Tao ba 'yan?
*EN: …Is that a person?*

**BEA**
Kanina pa po siya diyan.
*EN: It's been there the whole time.*

**NICO**
Kanina pa?
*EN: The whole time?*

He rewinds.

> **FOL:** the rewind whine (NEW: "VCR rewinding, motor whine rising, tape
> spooling"), then `vcr-play`.

**[4:3 TAPE]** The set-down again, then the countdown, "*Isa, dalawa…*" Nico
steps it frame by frame through **the flash**, and in the flash frame the doorway
is lit. The figure is there, during the photo, while everyone smiles.

**[2.39]** CLOSE on Nico. The CRT is reflected in both lenses of his glasses, and
in each lens is a tiny white doorway with a tiny dark shape in it.

> **FOL:** `breath-nervous`, from Nico, held.

He lets the tape play on.

**[4:3 TAPE]** The family comes back in, laughing and wet from the drizzle. They
walk past the kitchen doorway, inches from it, and nobody looks. JOJO stops right
in front of the camera, waves up at it with his paper plate and grins.

**JOJO**
Bye, Tay!
*EN: [Bye, Dad!]*

He runs off. Behind where he stood, the figure has not moved.

Someone switches off the tube light: the party is over. The room is dark except
for the street light through the jalousies. The figure is a thin outline, still
there. The tape runs on in the dark for a long time.

> **AMB (tape):** goodbyes off screen, a gate, then only tape hiss and the faint
> camcorder motor (`camcorder-room`).

Then the tape ends: blue screen.

> **FOL:** the VCR's end-of-tape clunk and auto-rewind (NEW: "VCR reaching end of
> tape, mechanism clunk, starts rewinding").

**[2.39]** Nico doesn't move. CLOSE on the PC: **CAPTURE_001 — COMPLETE.**

**BEA** (small)
Pa… ipo-post mo po ba 'yan?
*EN: Pa… are you going to post that?*

**NICO**
Hindi. …Ewan ko.
*EN: No. …I don't know.*

**BEA**
Huwag na po.
*EN: Please don't.*

A long beat.

**NICO**
Matulog ka na, anak.
*EN: Go to sleep, sweetheart.*

Bea turns on the bench, pulls his jacket up to her chin and lies **facing the dark
back doorway.**

> **AMB:** the fan **returns** to the mix (relief, briefly).

## SCENE 3 (3:38–4:32)

**[2.39] INT. SHOP — LATER**

The rain has softened to a patter. Bea is asleep.

Nico tears a strip of masking tape, writes **01** on it in marker and sticks it on
Tape 1.

> **FOL:** masking tape tearing and a marker squeak (NEW).

He props his phone against a stack of tapes, aimed at the CRT (still paused on the
doorway) and the counter. He taps record and leans close to whisper, putting on
his channel voice:

**NICO**
Tape one. Walang label. Walang pangalan.
*EN: Tape one. No label. No name.*

**He sets the phone down, still recording,** and turns to the PC.

> **FOL:** a phone set down on the stack of plastic cases (NEW), and a chair
> (`chair`).

At the PC he types into a search box: *Rudy despedida 1991 nawawala* ("missing").

CLOSE on the screen: an invented Facebook-style group, **NAWAWALA PA RIN** ("STILL
MISSING"). A scanned photo shows Rudy in that same new polo, the price tag on the
sleeve. The post reads:

> **Rodolfo "Rudy" — hindi nakasakay sa flight niya. Hunyo 1991.**
> **Naka-lock ang bahay mula sa loob. Walang nakakita.**
> *EN: Rodolfo "Rudy" never boarded his flight. June 1991.*
> *The house was locked from the inside. No one saw anything.*

Below it, one comment from 2019, signed **JoJo**:

> **Tay, uwi ka na.**
> *EN: Dad, come home.*

Nico sits back. He rubs his eyes under his glasses, then looks at the envelope of
old notes by the keyboard.

Then the flaw. He drags **CAPTURE_001** into an upload window on *Lumang Tape*. In
the title field he types **TAPE 01**. His cursor hovers over UPLOAD.

> **AMB:** the fan's sweep passes over him, once.

He clicks. A progress bar fills, and the screen reads **Na-upload na**
("Uploaded"). The view counter turns from 0 to 1.

## SCENE 4 (4:32–5:20)

**[2.39] INT. SHOP — NIGHT**

Nico picks up his phone, stops the recording and plays the clip back to trim it
for a teaser.

**[16:9 PHONE]** The bars come off. The clip is from a few minutes ago: the CRT
paused on the tape's doorway; Nico's back as he types at the PC; beyond him, Bea
asleep on the bench; and beyond her, **the shop's own back doorway**, dark.

The phone's picture **breathes**: soft, sharp, soft. The phone's autofocus is
hunting, the same as the tape's.

> **HOR:** the focus hunt again, now as a modern phone's tiny lens motor (NEW:
> "smartphone camera autofocus clicking and hunting, very faint"). `drone` comes in
> at the threshold of hearing.
> **AMB:** the world drops out one sound at a time: **the fan** goes, then **the
> rain** thins to nothing, and the CRT whine is the last sound left.

In the clip, in the shop's back doorway, just behind sleeping Bea, a **tall
figure stands perfectly still, facing the phone.** It has the same too-long arms
and the same paused-frame smear for a face.

**[2.39]** CLOSE on Nico, his expression frozen in fear. The phone's glow is in his
glasses.

He turns very slowly to look at the real doorway. A wide shot, locked off: the
bench, Bea asleep, the doorway. Black. Empty.

> **AMB:** the CRT whine **stops**. Total silence.

**[16:9 PHONE]** Back to the clip. Its timecode runs on. In the clip, Nico (minutes
ago) moves his hand to the mouse and clicks UPLOAD. **At that moment the figure's
head tilts,** slowly, to one side.

> **HOR:** one `tape-breath`, slow and warped, the only sound.

**[2.39]**

**NICO** (barely a breath)
Bea.
*EN: Bea.*

He crosses the shop on careful tsinelas.

> **FOL:** `step` (soft, slow).

He gathers Bea up in both arms and her head rolls onto his shoulder. She stays
asleep. He backs towards the shutter, eyes on the doorway the whole way, ducks
under the half-open shutter and is gone into the wet street.

> **FOL:** the shutter's rattle as his shoulder brushes it (NEW: "steel roll-up shop
> shutter rattles"). Outside, rain on asphalt, far away.

**[2.39] FINAL SHOT: the shop from inside, locked off.** The counter. The phone,
dropped face up, still playing the clip: a small glowing rectangle, and in it a
tiny figure in a tiny doorway. Beyond the phone, in the real frame, is the real
doorway, black.

Hold four seconds in silence. The audience searches the real doorway and finds
nothing.

The phone's screen times out and goes black. In the black, faintly, comes **the
focus hunt**: a tiny motor whirring, searching.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END CARD:** *TRANSFERS — 1 — "Bayad Na"*
