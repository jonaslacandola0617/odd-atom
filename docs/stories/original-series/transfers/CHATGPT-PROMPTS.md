# TRANSFERS: character and style sheets via the user's ChatGPT Plus (prompt pack v1, 2026-11-12)

The user offered their ChatGPT Plus for character sheets: "if you want to draft a character sheet for our
series we can definitely do that and anything else you want to do with it".

## How to use

1. Paste a prompt into ChatGPT (image generation).
2. Generate 2–3 versions of each, and keep your favourite.
3. Ask for changes in plain words ("make him thinner", "older", "less cute").
4. Put the picks in one Google Drive folder and share the link ("Anyone with
   the link: Viewer").
5. I build the 3D toon models from them. The sheets are design references; the
   final characters are our own models.

Every prompt starts with the same **style block**, so the whole cast matches.

## Style block (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Original character design for a dark Filipino horror animated series, rendered later as toon-shaded 3D with a pixel-art finish. Stylized but grounded, NOT cute or chibi: about 6 heads tall, simple bold shapes, clear readable silhouettes, slightly elongated limbs, simplified hands with 4 fingers and a thumb. Faces are simple and graphic so they can be drawn in pixels: strong eyebrows, clear eye shapes with small pupils, a simple nose, a simple mouth line. Clean cel-shaded illustration with flat colours, one shadow tone, and thick dark outlines. Muted, slightly desaturated palette with deep shadows. Present-day provincial Philippines. No text, logos or watermarks except small view labels.

## Sheet layout (add after the character description)

> Layout: a character turnaround sheet on a plain light-grey background. A full body in a relaxed neutral standing pose shown from front, three-quarter, side and back, all at the same scale and aligned on one ground line. Below, a row of 6 head close-ups showing the facial expressions listed. On the right, a column of 6 flat colour swatches of the character's palette.

## Characters

### 1. NICO (lead)

> Character: Nico, 34, Filipino man, owner of a small struggling video-transfer shop. Average height, lean with a slightly soft belly, rounded tired shoulders. Short messy black hair with a cowlick. Black rectangular glasses (his signature shape). Tired eyes with dark under-eye bags, heavy straight eyebrows, light stubble. Wears a faded dark-teal T-shirt, dark jeans, worn grey rubber shoes, a cheap black digital wristwatch on his left wrist. Decent, quiet, worn down by debt. Expressions: neutral tired, focused (narrowed eyes), forced polite smile, worried, frozen fear (eyes wide, unblinking), terror (mouth open).

### 2. LIZA (Nico's wife)

> Character: Liza, 33, Filipino woman, a nurse who works night shifts. Practical, sharp, protective, tired of the shop's debts. Shoulder-length black hair tied back loosely, a few strands loose. Wears faded light-blue scrubs, or at home a plain oversized shirt. A thin silver necklace with a small cross. Expressions: neutral, skeptical, angry, worried, comforting, fear.

### 3. BEA (their daughter)

> Character: Bea, 9, Filipino girl, quiet and observant, does homework in her father's shop after school. Straight black hair with a fringe, small hair clip. Oversized school uniform (white blouse, navy skirt), sneakers, a backpack with a small keychain charm. Big serious eyes. Must look like a real child, not cute or chibi. Expressions: neutral, curious, secretive, scared, crying, blank staring (unsettling).

### 4. MANG ERNING (the retired detective)

> Character: Ernesto "Mang Erning", 64, Filipino retired police detective. Thin and wiry, slightly stooped, grey crew-cut hair, deep lines, a grey moustache. Wears a faded short-sleeve barong-style polo or a checked polo, brown slacks, old leather shoes, a cheap ballpen in his shirt pocket, a folded handkerchief. Calm, dry humour, haunted by an unsolved case. Expressions: neutral, suspicious, wry half-smile, grave, shocked, exhausted.

### 5. TITO BOY (the wedding videographer, seen in the old tapes)

> Character: "Tito Boy", a Filipino wedding and party videographer as he looked in the late 1990s, about 40. Medium build, side-parted hair with gel, a thin moustache, a cheerful salesman's smile. A khaki multi-pocket photographer's vest over a polo shirt, a shoulder-mounted VHS camcorder with a small light on top. He is the red herring: friendly on the surface, something hidden in the eyes. Expressions: friendly grin, directing guests, tired, nervous, caught off guard, blank.

### 6. THE ONE WHO SETS THE CAMERA DOWN (the entity)

> Character design for a horror entity: a human-shaped figure that is only ever seen standing perfectly still in the dark corner of old home videos. Tall and slightly too thin, arms hanging too long and straight, head tilted a little forward. Its face is never clear: a blurred smear like a paused VHS frame, with only darker hollows where eyes should be. Dressed in dark, plain, slightly old-fashioned clothes that are hard to read. Its silhouette must be instantly recognisable and frightening, but simple. Show it front, three-quarter, side and back, plus 4 close-ups: the blurred face; the blurred face with the head tilted; the silhouette in a doorway lit from behind; and the figure half hidden behind a door frame. No gore.

## Locations (style frames)

### 7. THE SHOP (key location)

> Environment concept art for a small, cluttered video-transfer shop in a provincial Philippine commercial row at night. Steel roll-up shutter half down, a hand-painted sign. Inside: a counter with a CRT monitor and stacked VCRs, a glowing VCR clock, shelves crowded with labelled VHS and Hi8 tapes, a cardboard box of unlabelled tapes, a stand fan, a desk lamp, a calendar from a hardware store, cables everywhere, a dark doorway to a back room. Light: a warm desk lamp and the cold blue glow of the monitor, with rain outside. Toon-shaded, flat colours, thick outlines, muted palette with deep shadows, a pixel-art feel. Wide establishing view and a second view from behind the counter. No text except the sign.

### 8. A TAPE (style frame for the found footage)

> A single frame from an old 1990s Filipino family home video (a birthday party at night in a small living room with a cake and candles, family members seen from behind, a monobloc chair, a plastic tablecloth), shot on a VHS camcorder: soft focus, colour bleed, tracking lines, date stamp in the corner. In the dark corner of the room, barely visible, a tall still figure stands facing the camera. Same toon and pixel-art style, but degraded like VHS.

## Notes

- **Copying:** none of these prompts name a studio, artist or film. Keep it that
  way when you ask for changes (say "darker" or "more grounded", not "in the
  style of X").
- **Fixed traits:** the glasses, the watch, the teal T-shirt and the entity's
  blurred face are story-critical; please keep them through revisions.
- **Status:** prompts only. Nothing has been generated by me (I have no image
  generator); the user runs them.
