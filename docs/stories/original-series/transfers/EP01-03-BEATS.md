# TRANSFERS: beat sheet, Episodes 1–3 (draft v1, 2026-11-12, not approved)

Each episode has a different form and puts its scare somewhere else. In each
one, the figure stands closer to Nico's real life.

| | **Ep 1 "Bayad Na" (Paid in Full), 5:20** | **Ep 2 "Mas Malapit" (Closer), about 4:40** | **Ep 3 "Sino'ng Kumuha?" (Who Took It?), about 5:00** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form** | Real-time viewing in the shop at night | Forensic: acetate tracings on the CRT, tape after tape | Daylight: a doorstep investigation and one long interview |
| **Cold open** | Inside Tape 01: the despedida, the hand-over, the set-down and the flash. It cuts just before anything can be seen | Dawn at home: Liza back from her shift finds Nico asleep in his clothes. He lies about the money and Bea keeps his secret | Inside Tape 03, Noche Buena (1993): the camera is set down facing the parol window |
| **Want, and the flaw that pulls him in** | Money: ₱9,000 to copy nine tapes, and a channel that pays | He needs more uploads, and he has to know if it's real | Erning's case gives him a reason to keep going: "evidence" |
| **Rule learned** | 1. It appears only when no hand is on the camera | 2. It is closer in every tape, by date | 3. The one who set it down is gone and the house is locked from inside; the others forget who filmed |
| **Where the scare sits** | **At the end**, outside the tape, in his own phone clip | **In the middle**, inside Tape 02: the videoke scores 100, everyone turns to cheer, and the autofocus pulls past them onto the figure | **Dead centre, and no character sees it**: JoJo's daughter's dance video shows the figure behind Nico's chair |
| **Where it stands in Nico's world** | His shop's back doorway, behind sleeping Bea | (none; the dread is the stack of tracings) | Behind Nico's own chair, in someone else's house |
| **Sound idea** | The focus hunt is born; the world drops out one sound at a time; it ends in silence | The scoring jingle's fanfare under the focus pull (original); marker squeaks; the hum of the CRT | Almost no horror layer: the vulcanizing compressor, roosters and the street, so the one cut to the phone lands in near silence |
| **The turn** | Bea sees it first. Rudy never boarded his flight, his house locked from inside. Nico **uploads anyway** | The tracings stack up into a figure walking towards the glass | Mang Erning arrives with a printout of the upload. JoJo remembers everything except who was filming |
| **Cliffhanger (new information)** | The figure in his shop, on his phone, tilts its head as he clicks UPLOAD | Tape 03's dark TV screen reflects the cameraman, smiling, waving at himself | Erning lays down the 1999 "WANTED FOR QUESTIONING" notice with the same face: "Nine families. Nine tapes." |
| **Planted for later** | "*Ikaw naman*"; the airmail envelope; the old banknotes; "*Huwag mo munang sasabihin kay Mama*" | The figure always faces the lens, never the family | "My kid films everything these days" (it returns a generation later) |
| **Misdirection it builds** | The box is haunted | The man in the vest is doing it | He is a serial abductor, and maybe still alive |

**The escalation in one line:** in Episode 1 it is in a tape, then in his shop; in
Episode 2 it has a pattern and a suspect; in Episode 3 the police have a name,
while the audience alone sees it standing behind Nico.
