## Facts, if commenters ask

- Clark was a US air base until 1991; the hospital building was abandoned.
- Sources differ on the hospital's history (some say the Second World War, some the Vietnam era).
- It was featured by Ghost Hunters International and National Geographic's *I Wouldn't Go In There*, and Philstar reported a ghost-hunting craze there in 2005–06.
- The voices, stones, nurse and roadside woman in white are reported legends.
- Search summaries only; re-check before quoting dates.
