## The belief, if commenters ask

- The manananggal is a figure of Visayan folklore: a woman who separates at the waist at night, grows bat-like wings, and leaves her lower half behind.
- She is said to prey on sleepers, especially pregnant women, with a long, thin tongue.
- The traditional defence is salt, crushed garlic or ash on the open waist of the lower half, so she cannot rejoin it before sunrise.
- Details vary by region. This video retells the folklore; it is not a report of a real event.
