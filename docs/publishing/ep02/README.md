2. **The TV listing** (about 0:27): 11:00, channel 11, is already circled before he comes back.
3. **The wall clock** shows the reported times: **9:14** for the first intrusion (about 0:07) and **11:15** for the second (about 0:25).
4. **Two frames of the mask** hide in the closing static (about 0:55), with a two-frame buzz.

## Facts to have ready if commenters ask

- Sunday 22 November 1987. First intrusion: WGN-TV, about 9:14 pm, during the sports report; roughly 25 seconds, no voice, only a buzz. WGN's engineers switched the signal.
- Second intrusion: WTTW (channel 11), about two hours later, during a *Doctor Who* rerun; about 90 seconds, with distorted audio.
- The FCC investigated. No one has ever been identified or charged.
- The video is a recreation: the man, the apartment and the station rooms are illustrative. The mask, the audio, the hummed tune and the TV shows are original, not the real broadcast.
