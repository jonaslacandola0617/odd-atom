## Facts, if commenters ask

- The house was a council semi in Enfield, north London; activity was reported from August 1977 into 1979.
- A police officer reported seeing a chair move about three to four feet across the floor at about 1 a.m., and found no wires.
- Maurice Grosse and Guy Lyon Playfair recorded over 180 hours of tape; the voice on the tapes came from one of the daughters.
- SPR investigators Anita Gregory and John Beloff concluded the children faked activity; skeptics attribute the voice to false vocal cords; one daughter later said they faked it "once or twice". Grosse believed there was no trickery.
