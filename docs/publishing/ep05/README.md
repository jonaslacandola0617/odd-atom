## Facts to have ready if commenters ask

- Nine hikers led by Igor Dyatlov camped on the slope of Kholat Syakhl on the night of 1–2 February 1959.
- Searchers found the tent on 26 February, cut from the inside; footprints in socks and bare feet led downhill.
- The last four were found in a ravine in May.
- Russian prosecutors concluded in 2019–2020 that an avalanche was responsible. A 2021 study (Gaume & Puzrin, *Communications Earth & Environment*) modelled a delayed slab avalanche; its authors say not everything can be explained, and critics still dispute it.
