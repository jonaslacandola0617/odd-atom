## Folklore, if commenters ask

- The tikbalang is a tall creature with a horse's head and very long limbs; when it squats, its knees rise above its head. It haunts mountain roads, forests and bamboo.
- It leads travellers astray in circles. Remedies: turn your shirt inside out; say "tabi tabi po" when passing its places.
- Rain while the sun shines is "kasal ng tikbalang"; the kapre (a cigar-smoking giant) shares the big trees.
