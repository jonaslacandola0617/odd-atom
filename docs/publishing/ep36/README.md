## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** across the aisle, the dark window shows your bench full of riders; the bench across is empty.
- **Escalation:** the Ikot under the acacias; riders get off one by one and look back at you; one coin too many; the seat beside you sinks; in the window, a grey figure with its head on your shoulder looks up at you; the driver won't turn.
- **The reveal:** two grey handprints on the back of her jacket.
- **The loop:** the jeep passes empty, someone sitting in it in the puddle; then the window again.
- **Hidden details:** in the window the riders stay seated after they've got off; the driver's mirror is turned up to the roof; the handprints' fingers come over the shoulders.
