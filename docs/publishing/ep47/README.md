## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** the old plank wall at a child's height; small lights pass behind the gaps.
- **Escalation:** the old house up north; the gasera goes out and the voices begin; Lola: "huwag mong sasagutin".
- **The turn:** my name, called from the wall, the gaps lighting toward my head; my ear to the planks; I answer.
- **The reveal:** silence; Lola's voice through the boards, and an eye in the gap.
- **The stinger:** Lola beside me, eyes open, her head turned round to me too far.
- **The loop:** the lights behind the planks again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - one plank by the bed is broken low down, from the first shot of the room;
  - the voices stop the instant I answer;
  - the voices always come from the left, the wall's side, until Lola's voice.

## English subtitles

1. Every night, someone was talking inside the wall. But there was no one on the other side.
2. It was a holiday at Lola's old house up north. The walls were wood, the planks old.
3. When the lights went off, they'd start. Quietly. Like many small voices.
4. Lola said they were engkanto. I shouldn't answer them, even if they called me.
5. On the third night, someone called my name. From the wall.
6. I couldn't help it. I pressed my ear to the planks, and I answered.
7. Everything went quiet. Then someone whispered from the other side... in Lola's voice.
8. But Lola was asleep beside me. With her eyes open.
