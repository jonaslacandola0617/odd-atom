# After Sign-Off: posts for Season 5, Episodes 41–50 (YouTube Shorts and TikTok)

Season 5 retells Philippine personal horror experiences that people shared online, in our own words, with the user's PSX models, Taglish narration (Juanito) and Taglish captions. Slate: `docs/stories/season-5-philippines.md`. These are paste-ready drafts; nothing has been posted, and no view counts are guaranteed. Each episode's hidden details and moderation notes are in `docs/publishing/epNN/README.md`.

**Rules for every post:**
- Say "batay sa mga karanasang ibinahagi online"; claim nothing as verified.
- Never name, tag or link the original poster; no usernames, photos, exact places, employers, schools, hospitals, operators or plates.
- If a poster objects, the user decides whether to take the video down or credit them.
- No dares or "go there" framing.
- Post in Philippine evening hours (UTC+8): YouTube 8:00–10:00 p.m., TikTok 9:00–11:30 p.m.
