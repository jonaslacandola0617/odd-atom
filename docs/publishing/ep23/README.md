## Facts, if commenters ask

- John Bell died on 19 or 20 December 1820 (sources differ), in Robertson County, Tennessee.
- The account of a strange vial and the voice claiming it gave him "a dose" comes from M. V. Ingram's 1894 book, which quotes a manuscript by Richard Williams Bell that has never been found.
- Skeptoid and others argue Ingram may have invented the death story; an 1880 sketch differs from his account.
- Search summaries only; re-check before quoting.
