## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** three robed figures at a door across the street, through the jalousie; knock, knock, knock; the window lights.
- **Escalation:** windows lighting, then turning to candles, house by house; Nanay's chalk cross; the three stop in front of *our* house and one hood tilts up to the window; they pass; then three knocks from inside Lola's room.
- **The loop:** the last shot is the first shot; the three are back at the door across the street, about to knock.
- **Hidden details:** a candle already burns at the far end of the street in the first shot; Lola's door has a chalk cross too; the crickets stop while they stand outside.
