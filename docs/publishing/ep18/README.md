## Facts, if commenters ask

- Malacañang has been the seat of power since Spanish times (the governors-general from the 1860s, then the presidents).
- Kalayaan Hall in the complex was built in 1920–21 and now houses the Presidential Museum and Library.
- The stories (the light in the Quezon Room, pacing in times of crisis, smoke in the Roxas Room, a child, a headless priest in a 2016 employee account) are staff and family recollections reported in the press, not records.
- Not to be confused with UP's Kalayaan Residence Hall.
- Search summaries only.
