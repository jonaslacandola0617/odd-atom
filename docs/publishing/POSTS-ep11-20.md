# After Sign-Off: posts for Season 2, Episodes 11–20 (YouTube Shorts and TikTok)

Season 2 is Philippine ghost stories with Taglish narration (Juanito) and Taglish captions. Slate: `docs/stories/season-2-philippines.md`. These are paste-ready drafts; nothing has been posted, and no view counts are guaranteed. Each episode's hidden details, facts and moderation notes are in `docs/publishing/epNN/README.md`.

**Rules for every post:**
- Titles only promise what happens in the video.
- Reported legends are called legends ("kilalang urban legend"); folklore is told with respect, never as "superstitious".
- No real person is named as a ghost, victim or culprit.
- No "go there at 3 a.m." challenges: several places are homes, businesses or protected sites.
- Post in Philippine evening hours (UTC+8): YouTube 8:00–10:00 p.m., TikTok 9:00–11:30 p.m.
