## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** the torch counting sleepers in a dark room; the sixth, facing the wall.
- **Escalation:** the old building; 3 a.m.; the count, a click on every number.
- **The turn:** morning: no one sleeps up there; the mattress is bare and dusty.
- **The reveal:** the recount: still six, but the upper bunk is empty.
- **The stinger:** it lies behind me; its head comes round to my nape and over my shoulder at the lens.
- **The loop:** the count again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - in the recount, the last pool of light lands on my own bed, not the empty bunk;
  - the bunk creaks before the empty upper bunk is shown;
  - in the morning, all four friends are looking at the same mattress.

## English subtitles

1. Five of us rented bedspace in an old boarding house. But six were sleeping.
2. The building was old. Three double-decks to a room. We were the only tenants.
3. At three a.m. I woke up. I counted us with my phone's flashlight.
4. One, two, three, four, five... six. The one above my bed, facing the wall.
5. The next day they said no one sleeps up there. The mattress was all dust.
6. Last night I counted again. Still six. But it wasn't up there anymore.
7. It had moved. It was in my bed. Right behind me.
8. I could feel its breath on my nape. It was never facing the wall. It was facing me.
