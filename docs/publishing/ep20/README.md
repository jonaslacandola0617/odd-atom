## Facts, if commenters ask

- The Manila Film Center was rushed for the 1982 Manila International Film Festival.
- On 17 November 1981, at about 3 a.m., scaffolding collapsed and workers fell into the setting cement. The festival opened on schedule.
- The number of dead is disputed: a figure of seven was printed; rumours ran to 169; a 2005 documentary is reported to have traced the workers and found no more than about a dozen.
- The ghost stories (voices, figures, full seats) are reported legend.
- Search summaries only; re-check before quoting.
