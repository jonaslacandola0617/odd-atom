## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** seven flashlight beams in a concrete tunnel, going out one by one.
- **Escalation:** the island at night; the old hospital's bed bolts in the floor; a lateral sealed by rubble, dust trickling; "Patayin ang ilaw."
- **The black:** about seven seconds with no light; more footsteps than there are people; a bed frame creaks; a far bell.
- **The reveal:** the lights come back, "pareho pa rin kami. Halos." My beam swings back: a man in an old helmet, head bowed. The count: six, and a seventh.
- **The loop:** the beams in the lateral again.
- **Hidden details:** he is already in the first frame, by the left wall; seven beams for six visitors and a guide; the glow in the black is where he stood.
