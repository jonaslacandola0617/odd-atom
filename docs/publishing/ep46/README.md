## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** the conductor's punch counts up the aisle and stops on a woman behind me, head bowed.
- **Escalation:** the last trip north; a woman alone under a lamp at a curve by a ravine; she boards, dripping, and sits behind me.
- **The turn:** he comes to collect, and the seat is empty. The bus stops; he counts everyone: twenty-three.
- **The reveal:** the backrest is still pressed in, and wet. He counts again, stops at me, and looks behind me.
- **The stinger:** she comes over my seat at the lens.
- **The loop:** his count up the aisle again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - she is already behind me in the first second;
  - water drips from her hair onto the seat even in the first second;
  - it isn't raining anywhere outside.
