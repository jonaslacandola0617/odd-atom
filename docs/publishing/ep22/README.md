## Facts, if commenters ask

- The doll is kept at Mannenji temple in Iwamizawa, Hokkaido; the Suzuki family left it there in 1938 when they moved.
- The temple's account (as relayed) is that her hair, short at first, has grown to about 25 cm and grows back after trimming.
- The claim that it is a child's hair is unverified. Skeptics suggest the fibres relaxing or human intervention.
- Some sources give a 1918 purchase and other names and dates; these are unverified and not used.
