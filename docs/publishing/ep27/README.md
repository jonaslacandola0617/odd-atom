## Facts, if commenters ask

- **Dates:** the first building opened in 1910; the large building opened on 17 October 1926 and closed in 1961, after streptomycin made sanatoria obsolete.
- **The tunnel:** about 500 ft (the "body chute"), used to move supplies and the dead out of sight of the patients.
- **The death toll:** disputed. 63,000–64,000 is legend. One account cites an assistant medical director's autobiography for a highest yearly toll of 152; others give about 6,000 between 1926 and 1961.
- **Timmy:** investigators leave a ball for "Timmy" on the third floor; no record of the boy is cited.
- Search summaries only; re-check before quoting.
