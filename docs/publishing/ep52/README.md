## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** a man's face in a feathered body, in a work light, tipping its head at you.
- **Escalation:** a silent neighbour points up; the bucket rises; a feathered back; its head comes round to the light.
- **The gore:** its legs are still a man's legs; the skin splits as the quills push through.
- **The stinger:** the wings open at the lens; the phone falls.
- **The end:** a clip on his phone he never took, from the branch, looking down at him.
- **Hidden details:**
  - a curtain in the second house is pulled aside as the truck passes;
  - in the clip from the branch, he is looking up from the street before he went up;
  - the dogs stop barking before the truck parks.

## Captions (the burned-in English)

1. Two in the morning, I got a work order to cut the power to one house. Last street in the colonia.
2. A neighbour was waiting by the pole. She didn't say a word. She just pointed up, into the tree.
3. I went up in the bucket, filming. I thought it was the biggest owl I'd ever seen. Then it turned to the light. It had a man's face.
4. It wasn't finished changing. Those were still a man's legs. And everywhere the feathers were coming through... the skin was splitting open.
5. I drove off with the bucket still up. On my phone there's a second video. I didn't take it. It's from the branch. Looking down at my truck.
