## Folklore and science, if commenters ask

- The santelmo is a ball of fire seen at night over fields, swamps, rivers and the sea; it follows or leads travellers astray. Many say it is the soul of someone who drowned, or was never properly mourned. Remedies: turn your clothes inside out; a Mindoro tale uses vinegar, salt and water.
- The name comes from St. Elmo's fire: a real, documented glow (a corona discharge) on masts and points in stormy air. Ball lightning is reported but poorly understood; marsh gas has been proposed for lights over swamps. None explains a light that follows you.
- Search summaries only.
