## Folklore, if commenters ask

- The tiyanak is said to cry like a newborn in forests or tall grass to lure travellers, and to show its true form, small and ancient, when picked up.
- In the Spanish-period version, tiyanak are the souls of infants who died before baptism; the belief is older, and versions differ by region.
- A remedy for travellers led astray: wear your clothes inside out.
- Search summaries only (Wikipedia citing Eugenio's *Philippine Folk Literature*, and DZRH News).
