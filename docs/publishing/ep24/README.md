## Facts, if commenters ask

- Don Julián Santana Barrera lived on the chinampa from about the 1950s and hung dolls he found in the canals and in rubbish.
- He said a girl had drowned nearby; accounts differ on who she was, and some say he imagined her.
- He died in 2001; his nephew has said he was found drowned in the same canal.
- The island is now visited by tourists and run by his family.
- Search summaries only; re-check before quoting.
