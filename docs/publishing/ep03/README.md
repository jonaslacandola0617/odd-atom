## Facts to have ready if commenters ask

- Hinterkaifeck, a farmstead near Schrobenhausen, Bavaria. The killings happened on the night of 31 March 1922; the family was found on 4 April.
- Before that night, the farmer told neighbours about tracks from the forest with none leading back, missing house keys, a Munich newspaper nobody had bought, and footsteps in the attic.
- A previous maid had left months earlier, saying the house was haunted.
- In the days after, someone fed the cattle and smoke was seen from the chimney.
- Never solved; a 2007 review by police students concluded it could not be solved after so long.
