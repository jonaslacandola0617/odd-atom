Rewind the universe far enough, and classical cosmology reaches a singularity. But is that the universe’s first moment—or where our explanation runs out?

The evidence for a hot Big Bang is strong. The ultimate beginning is still an open question.

This animated explainer follows cosmic expansion, ancient microwave light and the first light elements back toward the limits of our models. Then we explore what “before the Big Bang” could mean: a beginning of time, a hypothetical bounce, inflation and speculative multiverse histories.

We also untangle a tempting picture: a tiny dot exploding into an enormous black room. Where did that room come from?

A quantum vacuum is not the absence of every field, state and rule. And a beautiful picture of an earlier universe is not evidence that it existed.

Could “first” itself be the wrong question?
