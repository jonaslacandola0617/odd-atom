## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** an old woman at the foot of your bed, her back to you; her head starts to come round.
- **Escalation:** Lola in the dream, warning; the flood the next morning; Lola keeping watch.
- **The turn:** the last dream: her back to me; "pagbuksan mo ako ng pinto"; in the house, feet under the front door.
- **The reveal:** her head turns round first, then the body: Lola's rosary, not Lola's face.
- **The stinger:** awake, the front door open; the dent at the foot of the bed; she is sitting there.
- **The loop:** the woman at the foot of the bed again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - Lola faces me in her dream; the other one always shows me her back first;
  - the music box plays Lola's tune slower in the last dream;
  - the dent in the house is exactly where she sat in every dream.
