## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** in the mirror, a pale hand stroking my hair. I'm alone at the sink.
- **Escalation:** the dark corridor, the one lit CR door; the flickering tube; in the mirror, every cubicle door shut.
- **The turn:** in the room nothing touches me, but my head dips with every stroke. A door creaks somewhere behind me.
- **The reveal:** in the mirror the last cubicle is open, and the arm comes all the way across the room from inside it.
- **The stinger:** her head comes out of the cubicle, tipped onto its side, then snaps upright at the lens.
- **The loop:** the hand on my hair again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - she is already standing in the open cubicle in the first second;
  - the light flickers on the same beat as the buzz;
  - in the room the last door is always shut.
