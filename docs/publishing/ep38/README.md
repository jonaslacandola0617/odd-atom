## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** an old phone buzzing in a hand: "1 message received".
- **Escalation:** the town lighting up phone by phone; forwards that contradict each other; a woman at a market stall with her hair over half her face; the inbox at 99; a mother barring the door; an unknown number.
- **The reveal:** "Diin kamo? Ara ko sa gwa." Then, between the jalousie slats, half a face and a scar.
- **The loop:** the phone buzzes again.
- **Hidden details:** each forward adds a FWD:; the place changes in every one; a window goes dark when the door is barred.
