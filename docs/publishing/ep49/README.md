## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** a lit milk tea shop at night; the queue's heads come round to you, the bodies don't.
- **Escalation:** the 2 a.m. booking; inside, nobody moves; the crew's smile and the hand-over.
- **The turn:** walking out, every head is turned round to me.
- **The reveal:** by day the shutter is padlocked, closed for six months.
- **The stinger:** the receipt is from last night, and on its back: come back, the queue is one short. She is at the glass.
- **The loop:** the queue turning again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - there are five in the queue, and the gap at its end is my place;
  - the whole queue breathes in and out at the same moment;
  - the receipt reads 02:00 AM, the time of the booking.
