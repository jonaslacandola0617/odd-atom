## Facts to keep straight in replies

- Saturday 26 November 1977, about 5:10 pm, during the early evening news on Southern Television, ITV's region for the south of England.
- Only the **sound** was replaced, for about six minutes, and only on the **Hannington transmitter** (north Hampshire). The picture continued normally.
- The voice called itself Vrillon of the "Ashtar Galactic Command" and told people to give up their weapons and live in peace; it said there was little time.
- Southern Television apologised to viewers; the Independent Broadcasting Authority called it a hoax. **Nobody was ever identified or charged.** Do not name or hint at anyone in replies.
- The "Vrillon" name itself is how the event is remembered; some editors question whether early 1977 reports used it.
