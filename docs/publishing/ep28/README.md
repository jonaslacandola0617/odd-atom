## Facts, if commenters ask

- **The photograph:** 19 September 1936, Raynham Hall, Norfolk. The photographers were Captain Hubert C. Provand and his assistant Indre Shira, of Country Life.
- **Their account:** Shira said he saw "a vapoury form gradually assuming the appearance of a woman" coming down the stairs. Provand took the exposure and said he saw nothing.
- **Publication:** Country Life, December 1936 (sources give 16 or 26 December); Life magazine, January 1937.
- **The debate:** the negative was examined at the time and reported untouched. Joe Nickell and others argue double exposure; grease on the lens has also been suggested.
- **Earlier sightings:** a "Brown Lady" was reported on the stairs from the 1830s (legend).
- Search summaries only; re-check before quoting.
