## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** a dark van window; in it my face, and right beside me two pale women, heads turned to me.
- **Escalation:**
  - the van alone on a night avenue;
  - everyone sways at a pothole, but the two women don't move at all;
  - my phone game, with her pale hand on her thigh, still; she takes one breath;
  - in the window, their heads turn to me in jerks, too far.
- **The turn:** I look; the two seats are empty, still dented. The van drives on; nobody looks up. My hand on the warm vinyl.
- **The reveal:** the window again: they are in it, nearer, at my shoulder; the nearer one's head tips to the lens.
- **The loop:** the window of the first second.
- **Hidden details:**
  - in the first frame the nearer woman already has long hair over her face, unlike the woman in the van;
  - the two never sway at any bump;
  - the dents are on the seats before I turn.
