## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** the glass window of a coffin, fogging from the inside.
- **Escalation:** the wake by the hour, fewer each time, the camera lower each time; the candles lean toward the coffin; the cloth slides off the mirror and in the mirror the lid is open; three strikes; the body standing; it turns to you.
- **The release:** one top-down chase, you zigzag, it goes straight through the chairs.
- **The loop:** dawn, then the glass again, already fogging.
- **Hidden details:** the mirror shows the lid open while the room's is shut; the flames lean toward the coffin at 11; at dawn the chairs it hit are still down, with mud on the tiles.
