## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** 3 a.m., the elevator reaches the lobby, a ding, the doors open on an empty car.
- **Escalation:** the CCTV of the closed 14th floor, night by night:
  - empty;
  - someone at the far end;
  - right at the doors, her head turning round to the camera.
- **The turn:** "Kagabi, wala na siya sa ikalabing-apat." The indicator counts down to L.
- **The reveal:** the guard stands aside. The doors open on nothing; on the lobby camera she walks out past him and looks up at the lens.
- **The loop:** the doors opening in the first second.
- **Hidden details:**
  - the floor list has no 13;
  - on night 2 she steps closer between seconds;
  - the 14F camera's clock never passes 03:00.
