- **Escalation:**
  - gamelan where no one lives;
  - my friend walking in time;
  - she is nearer each time the torch swings back;
  - her feet hang above the leaves and her arms reach too long;
  - the stream.
- **The end:** she is standing over him in the water, and on the last beat she comes at the light.
- **Hidden details:**
  - she is past the gate from the very first frame;
  - my friend's walk is on the music's beat;
  - the insects go quiet each time she is in the light.

## Captions (the burned-in English)

1. The village gave us one rule. Never go past the stone gate into the forest. At midnight, past the gate, someone started playing gamelan.
2. My friend was already walking toward it. He wouldn't answer me. He was walking in time.
3. Every time my torch came back to the trees, she was there. Dancing. Slowly. A little closer.
4. Her feet never touched the ground. And her arms kept reaching, further than arms should go.
5. I found him at the stream. His arm was black and swollen with bites. And something was moving under his sarong.
6. The elders never blamed her. They blamed us. We broke the rule. I still don't know if he came back.
