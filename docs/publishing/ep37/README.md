**Folk belief** (barang, Siquijor) and a real tradition (the Holy Week healers). Tell barang as a belief; the healers are healers. Siquijor is not a "witch island": keep that label out of titles and captions.

## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** a bamboo tube's cork lifting by itself, and legs in the gap.
- **Escalation:** at insect scale: the beetle crawls out; on the sill at Good Friday dusk it opens its wings; the flight under a door; his sleeping face, the ear; his arm, something moving under the skin.
- **The turn:** the healers' cauldron on the mountain; the medicine at his lips; silence; a click of wings inside him.
- **The release:** he coughs; a beetle on its back on the mat. Then the tube again.
- **Hidden details:** the name on the paper is only a scribble; the bell never moves.
