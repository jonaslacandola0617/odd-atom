## Facts, if commenters ask

- In October 2003, on three consecutive days, security staff at Hampton Court Palace were called to close a fire door near Clock Court that kept opening.
- On the first day the CCTV showed the doors flying open with no visible cause; on another day a costumed or robed figure appeared and closed them.
- The palace released the footage in December 2003; Historic Royal Palaces has said there is no explanation.
- Suggested explanations include a visitor in costume or a prank.
