## Facts to have ready if commenters ask

- The light on Eilean Mòr, Flannan Isles, was seen to be out by the steamer *Archtor* on 15 December 1900.
- Bad weather kept the lighthouse tender *Hesperus* away until 26 December. There was no flag on the flagstaff, and nobody met the boat.
- The relief keeper found the station empty: the clocks stopped, the fire unlit for some days, and one set of oilskins left behind.
- The Northern Lighthouse Board's superintendent found storm damage at the west landing far above the sea. He suggested two keepers had gone down to secure equipment in the storm and the third had run after them; a large wave was thought to have swept them away. The men were never found.
- Search summaries only; re-read the superintendent's report before quoting details.
