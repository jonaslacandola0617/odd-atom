## Facts, if commenters ask

- The castle was built by the Dutch East India Company between 1666 and 1679; it is the oldest colonial building in South Africa.
- **Governor Pieter Gijsbert van Noodt:**
  - The legend: he sentenced seven soldiers to death (for desertion, by most accounts) in 1729, some say 1728; one cursed him from the gallows, and he was found dead at his desk.
  - Other accounts give a heart attack.
- **Reported:**
  - footsteps and voices, especially in the dungeon;
  - a woman in grey running through the corridors;
  - a tall man leaning over the battlements;
  - a woman's remains found during excavations, with some saying the sightings became rarer after.
- Search summaries only, mostly tourism pages; re-check before quoting.
