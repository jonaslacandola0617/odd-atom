# After Sign-Off: posts for Season 4, Episodes 31–40 (YouTube Shorts and TikTok)

Season 4 is local Philippine horror (a documented case, folklore, reported hauntings and legends) with Taglish narration (Juanito) and Taglish captions. Slate: `docs/stories/season-4-philippines.md`. These are paste-ready drafts; nothing has been posted, and no view counts are guaranteed. Each episode's hidden details, facts and moderation notes are in `docs/publishing/epNN/README.md`.

**Rules for every post:**
- Titles only promise what happens in the video.
- Documented cases are told "ayon sa mga ulat"; legends are called legends; folklore is told with respect.
- No real private person is named or shown; no photographs of real people.
- No "go there at night" challenges.
- Post in Philippine evening hours (UTC+8): YouTube 8:00–10:00 p.m., TikTok 9:00–11:30 p.m.
