## What the cut is built for

- **First second:** the house from above; three knocks at my door; the gap under it lights.
- **Escalation:** Lolo's wake. Then, night by night on the plan, the knocking comes nearer: the front door, the kitchen door, Mama and Papa's door.
- **The turn:** from my bed, the light under my door and the shadows of two feet. "Huwag daw buksan."
- **The reveal:** at the end of the hall, Lolo with his back to me; his head turns round, his body doesn't; then he's nearer.
- **The loop:** the three knocks at my door again.
- **Hidden details:**
  - each night the knocks start at a door one room nearer mine;
  - the feet under my door are bare;
  - his photo at the wake is turned dark.
