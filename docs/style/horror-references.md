# Look references for the pixel-3D analog-horror channel

Gathered 2026-11-10 by web search; these are pointers to study, not assets to copy. No footage was downloaded.

**Finding:** no channel found that already does *narrated horror stories* in PS1-style low-poly 3D with VHS damage. The look lives mostly in indie games, and the format lives in analog-horror web series. Combining the two is the channel's gap. Search terms that surface more: "PS1 horror no commentary", "haunted PS1", "low poly horror short", "analog horror EAS".

## The look (PS1 low-poly + VHS)

| Reference | What to study | Fits story |
|---|---|---|
| **Haunted PS1 Demo Disc** (2020, 2021 anthologies; free on itch.io) | Many artists' takes on low-res textures, fog, big polygons. The widest single sample of the look | all |
| **Puppet Combo**: *Nun Massacre*, *Murder House*, *Stay Out of the House* | The origin of the VHS + PS1 horror wave: tape grain, colour bleed, CRT framing. Very gory and 18+, so study the frame treatment, not the content | 6 (farmhouse), 9 |
| ***No One Lives Under the Lighthouse*** | A PS1-style lighthouse keeper story told almost without words: hard pixels, dithering, slow dread | 3 (Flannan Isles) |
| ***Iron Lung*** (David Szymanski) | Claustrophobic submarine horror with a tiny low-res view through instruments | 10 (sonar) |
| ***Mouthwashing*** | Shows low poly can look art-directed: high contrast, limited palette, deliberate framing | all (character scenes) |

## The format (analog-horror web series)

| Reference | What to study | Fits story |
|---|---|---|
| **Local 58** (Kris Straub) | Emergency-broadcast cards, hijacked TV, instructions to the viewer. Widely cited as the genre's origin | 8 (moon alert), 1, 2 |
| **Gemini Home Entertainment** | Educational VHS tapes that turn sinister | 7 (rules) |
| **The Walten Files** | Low-fidelity 3D animation with a VHS treatment: the closest 3D web series | look + pacing |
| **Petscop** | A fictional PS1 game recorded as a Let's Play; story hidden inside game footage | possible original series format |
| **The Mandela Catalogue**, **The Monument Mythos** | Pacing, silence, text cards, audio stingers | all |

## How to collect

Screenshot frames you like from no-commentary playthroughs and trailers on YouTube or Steam. Note what you like in each: resolution, colour, fog, text style, camera, texture. Then drop them in a Drive folder and send the link; this session can download Drive files. Those notes matter more than the images themselves.
