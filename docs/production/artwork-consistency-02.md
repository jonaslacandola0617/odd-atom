# Artwork consistency test 02: medicine and cyber

Two new static scenes extend the approved drawing direction into new subjects. The user has passed the previous artwork and character animation; these new illustrations are provided for review. Environment and object animation remain a later discussion.

## Medicine: a tiny infection, a large defense

[Download the full-size PNG](https://github.com/jonaslacandola0617/odd-atom/raw/refs/heads/main/assets/scenes/artwork-consistency-02/medicine-immune-defense.png)

![Medicine scene with a new clinician and a magnified immune-defense view](../../assets/scenes/artwork-consistency-02/medicine-immune-defense.png)

A new older clinician with silver curls examines a specimen in a warm, detailed clinic. Her microscope connects to an enlarged view of an immune cell surrounding microbes among red blood cells. Hands, microscope controls, slide, coat cuffs, specimen dish, workbench, window and distant landscape provide connected visual detail.

The character is a new person, with a different age, hair silhouette, complexion, clothing and posture. The drawing carries over the approved compact oval eyes, rounded cheeks, shaped hair masses, readable hands and constructed clothing. The enlarged biological view gives the subject a clear visual action. It is a stylized medical illustration rather than a measured microscopy image.

## Cyber: containing an intrusion

[Download the full-size PNG](https://github.com/jonaslacandola0617/odd-atom/raw/refs/heads/main/assets/scenes/artwork-consistency-02/cyber-network-defense.png)

![Cyber environment with network traffic, gateway, server city and isolated threat basin](../../assets/scenes/artwork-consistency-02/cyber-network-defense.png)

A router and circuit-board landscape becomes a miniature network world. Foreground Ethernet sockets and cable loops lead toward an amber gateway, mint data pathways and a distant server city. Coral fragments take a separate curved channel into an isolated basin. This is a visual metaphor for filtering and containment, with no human characters.

Rounded infrastructure, curved bridges, layered circuit channels, cooling structures and quieter distant buildings establish scale and depth. Mint/cream traffic, amber protection and coral threats have distinct visual roles. The composition centers the gateway and branching routes, with smaller details supporting them.

## Consistency review

[Compare with the approved scene and character crops](artwork-consistency-02/comparison.jpg) · [Initial-to-final refinement crops](artwork-consistency-02/refinement.jpg)

The approved original character sheet and movie poster were supplied as image references. Both new scenes received one refinement pass after inspecting the first generation:

- **Medicine:** the first red blood cells had granular/pitted surface texture, and the immune cell had many small convolutions. The final uses clean red discs, broad shaded planes and larger readable immune-cell lobes. The clinician's face, curls, clothing, hands and pose remain consistent with her initial scene design.
- **Cyber:** the first hardware had more glossy bevels, textured light and volumetric highlights than the baseline. The final uses broader illustrated planes, a simpler gate field and cleaner engineered contours. The routes, connectors, city and containment composition are retained.
- **Shared drawing rules:** curved silhouettes, deliberate overlapping forms, controlled shaded color planes, warm focal accents against cooler structure, and foreground/midground/background separation. Both final illustrations are free of lettering, captions, logos and production labels.
- **Differences to judge:** the clinic adds complex organic forms and a new human design; the network introduces more angular repeated hardware and a cooler dominant palette. The cyber scene contains no human drawing, so character consistency is assessed in the medicine scene. These boards support visual judgment; no numeric quality or studio-equivalence score is assigned.

The comparison board uses proportional full-scene thumbnails and native-pixel detail crops. Labels appear outside the diagnostic images. No retouching, color filters, cropping or upscaling were applied to the delivered PNGs. Their generated source files were copied byte-for-byte.
