## The belief, and care

- **Folklore:** the manananggal, widely recorded in Visayan lore, is a woman by day who separates at the waist at night, grows bat-like wings and leaves her lower half standing. She hunts sleepers, especially pregnant women, with a long, thin tongue. The defence is to cover the open waist of the lower half with **salt, crushed garlic or ash**, so she cannot rejoin it before sunrise. Checked from search summaries only (Wikipedia-derived pages, The Aswang Project).
- **Care:**
  - "Sa Visayas", never a named town or province (no Capiz, which has long carried an unfair aswang stigma);
  - **the pregnant woman and the baby are never harmed**: the tongue stops above her, and she is asleep and safe at dawn;
  - the waist is a clean black void;
  - her end is off-screen, as a far sound;
  - the neighbour by day is ordinary and kind; the horror is that you can't tell.
