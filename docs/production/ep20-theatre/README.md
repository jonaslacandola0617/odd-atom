## Care

- A real accident with real dead and grieving families.
- **No worker is shown at any time**, falling, trapped or dead. 1981 is an empty scaffold, work lights, a clock, a hard hat on a plank, then black.
- **No crash is sounded.** The scaffold creaks as it sways, then the picture cuts to black and the sound to total silence.
- The seated figures are dust-grey backs, never faces, never wounds; one turns its head only a little, and no face is ever seen.
- The toll is told as disputed; the entombment is not stated.
- No living person is named; the government of the day is not mentioned; nothing political.
- The last line honours the workers: "Baka naghihintay lang sila na banggitin ang mga pangalan nila." ("Maybe they're just waiting for their names to be said.")
