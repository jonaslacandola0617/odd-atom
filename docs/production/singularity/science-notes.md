# Scientific scope and source access

The episode concerns the origin of our observable universe and the classical Big Bang singularity. It must not claim an experimentally observed zero-size object, a proved creation from philosophical nothing, a discovered multiverse or a common starting singularity for every universe.

| Claim | Treatment in this draft |
|---|---|
| Large-scale expansion and an early hot, dense state | Supported cosmology. Say “on the largest scales” and “generally” so local gravitationally bound systems and approaching galaxies are not excluded. |
| Microwave background | Relic radiation that last scattered as the universe became transparent, approximately 380,000 years into the standard cosmic history; not light emitted at an observed singularity. |
| Light-element abundances | Hydrogen, helium and deuterium support hot early-universe nucleosynthesis. Do not imply every primordial abundance problem is resolved. |
| Age near 13.8 billion years | Standard cosmological estimate; not a direct observation of ultimate creation. |
| Infinite density/temperature | Classical extrapolation of the simple standard hot cosmological model. Singularities are more generally associated with spacetime incompleteness; not every mathematical singularity is a literal object or infinite-density point. |
| Quantum gravity | A complete experimentally established description of the relevant extreme regime is unavailable. Do not claim the singularity is proved absent, that infinity always means an equation is wrong, or that a named theory has already resolved the origin. |
| Small region versus entire universe | Our present observable region was much smaller in the hot past. The total universe may be larger or spatially infinite; expansion does not require a center or external container. |
| Beginning of time | One possible type of model. Do not assert that “before” is necessarily meaningless in all origin models. |
| A cosmic bounce | A speculative class of alternatives. No observed prior contraction or demonstrated bounce in our history is claimed. |
| Inflation | A proposed early accelerated expansion phase with open mechanism/model questions. Some scenarios motivate multiverse pictures; neither a generic ultimate-origin explanation nor a directly observed family of universes. |
| Quantum vacuum | A physical state within an assumed framework, distinct from the absence of all fields, laws, states, space and time. |
| Future evidence | Precise CMB, large-scale structure and potential primordial gravitational-wave evidence may test models. Do not claim a primordial gravitational-wave background or unique proof of our ultimate origin has been detected. |
| Elements in the viewer | Hydrogen traces to early cosmic history; much carbon/oxygen was produced through stellar evolution. Avoid saying all atoms formed in the Big Bang or all elements required supernovae. |

Live retrieval of all three URLs was attempted on 2026-10-24 and returned proxy **403 Forbidden**. Their current contents were not inspected in this session. These are background reading links, not a claim of fresh page verification or citations establishing speculative origin scenarios. The distinctions above are editorial checks based on established cosmology; a fresh literature review and completed external source audit are not claimed.

Any late-breaking observational discovery, named bounce mechanism, specific inflation model, or claimed quantum creation scenario added later needs its own accessible primary source and corresponding script revision. The present draft deliberately does not depend on such a new result.
