| 1 | “Rewind the universe” / immediate hook | A recognisable cosmos runs backward: large-scale expansion reverses, stars un-form, atoms ionize. | 4 | “A glowing dot… an enormous black room” / dismantle the misconception | Briefly show the tempting incorrect diagram as an imagined model. Pull attention to the invented surrounding room; replace it with expansion across a large field with no marked center. | Diagram seed and invented container; repeated galaxy field and moving distances. Do not leave the initial wrong diagram uncorrected. |
| 5 | “Everything we can see” / observable versus entire cosmos | Mark one observational region within a continuing landscape of galaxies; rewind that region while leaving the larger extent unspecified. Avoid depicting a measured physical edge of all existence. | Observation mask, independent far galaxy layers, scale changes. |
| 6 | “We need them to work together” / raise the stakes | Transition between particle-scale quantum imagery and spacetime geometry, then an intentionally unresolved shared regime. Avoid a fake solved quantum-gravity mechanism. | Particle groups, spatial geometry, overlapping conceptual regions and camera staging. |
| 7 | “Time has a beginning” / first possibility | Present an illustrative history with an initial boundary and no drawn earlier events. Clearly identify it as a possibility. | Timeline region, boundary, restrained camera approach. The graphic is an analogy, not a measurement. |
| 8 | “An earlier phase” / alternative history | A separate hypothetical branch shows contraction reaching a finite minimum and expansion following. Use different staging from the singularity so the viewer cannot mistake them for the same mechanism. | Matter field, scale envelope, transition and expansion. No claim that a bounce is observed. |
| 9 | “Inflation… other universes?” / broaden carefully | Abstract rapid expansion, then a clearly hypothetical larger structure containing disconnected regions. Do not draw other universes as surveyed planets or give them known physical properties. | Region boundaries, expansion fields, distinct foreground/background conceptual layers. |
| 10 | “Be careful with that word” / reveal hidden assumptions | An apparently empty physical setting reveals underlying fields/rules. Contrast a physical vacuum with the absence of a specified framework; do not animate particles as proof of creation from literal nothing. | Quiet field layers and conceptual framework reveal. |
| 11 | “You are already carrying part of this history” / emotional payoff and callback | Follow early hydrogen and later stellar element production into matter associated with a human. Return through the opening cosmic rewind and hold at the unresolved origin. | Original human artwork, separate body/gesture layers, elements, stars, gaseous environment, deep cosmos. Use the approved artwork quality and original character direction. |
