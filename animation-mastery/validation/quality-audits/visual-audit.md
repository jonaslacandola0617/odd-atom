## Comparison findings

[Character construction plate](../../analysis/visual-comparisons/character-construction-comparison.jpg): C01 has broad clean filled face/hair planes, substantial cheek patches, pupil-only oval eyes and a very simple mouth. Mira preserves her approved distinct identity with a smaller nose/mouth treatment, asymmetrical fringe, hoodie and softer painted texture. The face crops are resized for comparison; Mira is enlarged from the 1080p original movie. Their differing content/pose/resolution makes pixel-error scoring unsuitable as an art-quality measure. We have preserved one approved original identity, not demonstrated that arbitrary new identities can be generated at equal quality.

[Root explanation plate](../../analysis/visual-comparisons/root-explanation-comparison.jpg): source R01 fills the close frame with large rock planes, layered root contours, textured darkness and warm particles near contact. Our B uses a wider readable diagram with a more isolated obstacle and destination. It communicates a detour but lacks the source's dense organic interaction. It should not be sold as reconstruction of the source's fracture/mineral mechanism.

[Palette evidence](../../analysis/visual-comparisons/frame-local-palettes.jpg): pastoral, emotional and technical examples have different hue/contrast distributions. Extracted colors are quantized flattened-frame approximations; full-frame background dominance can hide small character colors. Creator/farmer semantic regions are recorded separately. These samples support local color relationships, not a universal studio palette.
