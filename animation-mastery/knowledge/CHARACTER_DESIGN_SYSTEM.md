# Character construction and identity

Evidence: [C01, D01, D02, D03, W01, T01](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md). Source files are flattened movies. They show visible construction and consistency; they do not disclose the actual authoring format or exact rigs.

## Head, face and hair

C01's hair has a dark rear silhouette and a brighter asymmetrical lobed front mass. Skin fills the intervening continuous face/ears/neck area. Eyes are dark filled ovals with no visible sclera in this view. Brows are thick rounded marks; the nose is a small asymmetric bean; cheek patches and a filled curved mouth provide expression. These elements remain spatially coherent while the hand and lighting change.

At native f2159 the selected skin mask bounds are `[1002,347,1589,836]`. That includes ears/neck and is **not** an authored head box. Left eye mask is 74×79 px, right 73×79 px; eye-center separation is about 218.24 px. Separation/skin-mask width is **0.37178** and left-eye-width/skin-mask-width **0.12606**. See [annotated geometry](../analysis/composition-diagrams/creator-face-geometry.png). These are local measurements for this face/view, not a universal facial template. The cropped shot cannot establish a full-body head ratio.

D01 provides a counterexample to universal proportions. The carrier has thin long bare legs, a tapered patterned dress and a much smaller head relative to total standing height than a chest-up creator might suggest. The bending worker has a compact, bearded face with torso/arm overlaps appropriate to the action. D02 suppresses most facial planes under green light; circular glasses and light-edged mouths carry identity. D03 is a bird whose beak, wing and feet require another construction family entirely.

## Torso, clothing and limbs

In D01 the basket bridges the carrier's curved forearms. Arm roots disappear into the clothing mass; fingers are simplified into a connected hand shape. Clothing uses large base planes plus sparse patterns/belt/collar boundaries. Bare limbs taper and feet extend beyond the leg; joints are not circles placed visibly between rectangular pieces. The bending worker's near arm and basket occlude the torso/leg region. That overlap is part of the drawing, not a cosmetic fix after animation.

T01 isolates hand shapes at larger scale. A lower-left hand supports the tablet while another approaches from upper right; the contact remains readable because warm hands surround an even brighter tablet on a dark field. We have not established a completed release or a library of hand variants from this case. W01's small walkers establish broad alternating silhouettes, but cannot justify close-up foot mechanics.

## Original character contract

Our approved original character is **Mira**, with Sol available in the existing atlas. Preserve the master hash and atlas registration recorded in [asset catalog](../assets/characters/catalog.json). Mira's purple asymmetrical hair, pink face, yellow hoodie, purple trousers and cream shoes are fixed identity anchors. This session did not generate a new raster character or trace a source character. It extended performance on the approved asset.

For each future identity, deliver before animation:

1. Clean full silhouette and face at intended screen size, with approved palette/material treatment.
2. Named registered layers/skin regions, hidden overlap artwork and exact local coordinates.
3. Neutral plus action-specific expression/hand/foot drawings. Keep facial anchors registered; do not scale the whole face to imitate an expression.
4. Required angles and extreme poses. A front/three-quarter raster cannot be made into a reliable side view by shrinking its width.
5. A rendered contact/extension test showing the actual action envelope. Approval applies to that envelope, not every possible pose.

## Current limits

The engine has registered facial alternatives and blink overlays, not a complete facial acting library, mouth/lip-sync system, three-angle turnaround or proven locomotion library. The new studies keep Mira's feet planted. Hidden surfaces are limited to the original preparation. Large turns/reaches require new authored artwork and another visual review. Do not describe those missing assets as implemented because the hierarchy or IK helper exists.
