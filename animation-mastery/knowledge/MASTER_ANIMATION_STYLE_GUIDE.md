# Production style specification, version 1

Read the [evidence index](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) first. This specification draws on selected native temporal/construction cases across all five videos and full-duration storyboard navigation. It is a practical starting system with measured examples, not a claim that every source shot has been reconstructed.

## Shared construction principles supported by the cases

**Build a readable silhouette before adding internal detail.** C01's face is a continuous pink mass bounded by two broad hair masses. D01's carrier remains legible through long exposed legs, dress silhouette and a basket bridging both arms. D03's bird is recognizable through head/beak, body, wing and feet before keyboard keys or chair buttons are considered. These drawings are simplified deliberately, but are not interchangeable circles with stick limbs. Curvature changes, broad lobes, tapered extremities and overlaps establish identity.

**Separate shapes through color and overlap rather than a universal outline.** C01 has no continuous dark contour around face/shirt; the collar and hair boundary provide separation. D01 uses dark hair/beard and cyan clothing against warm skin. R01 uses strong layered yellow/magenta root edges against cool rock. An internal diagram can therefore have a strong contour where a face does not. There is no measured universal pixel stroke width. Preserve local contrast at final frame size; do not impose black strokes on every object.

**Reserve the highest contrast for the explanatory event.** In C01 the core and face dominate the blue field. D02 concentrates green light on people/device while wall circuits stay dark. In L01 white plate edges and the amber ray isolate a functional layer of the leaf. These are different palettes implementing the same attention hierarchy.

**Give detail a scale hierarchy.** F01's large foreground leaf silhouettes, medium path/tree and smaller rear scenery create depth. D01's fields repeat crop masses while the basket/face has specific readable details. R01 uses broad faceted rocks and fine subdued soil. Fine marks should describe material or scale; they must not compete with the event.

## Contextual families; do not average them

| Family | Concrete references | Construction and color relationship | Appropriate original use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human/pastoral | D01; Civilization storyboard003–008 | Warm skin/crops, cyan garments, lime/teal land, rounded hills and patterned garments | Labor, society, consequences for people |
| Intimate/emotional | C01 | Large facial shapes, blue/cyan field, violet hair, luminous focal prop | Reaction and personal stakes |
| Technical/ominous | D02, A05–A06 | Dark navy masses, selective green/cyan paths, magenta agents, sparse bright labels | Network access, incentives, agency |
| Botanical/mechanism | G01, H01, L01, R01 | Strongly separated colored layers, repeatable cells, transparent overlays, warm active agents against cool structure | Cutaways, comparisons, flow and contact |
| Environmental depth | F01 | Overlapping foliage silhouettes, warm opening, cool framing, unequal layer motion | Establishing place and revealing a destination |

The [palette plate](../analysis/visual-comparisons/frame-local-palettes.jpg) and JSON contain measured local approximations. They are not authored source swatches. Full-frame frequency overweights the background: the creator/farmer samples therefore also record a semantic crop. Do not take a dark dominant color as the character's skin color or average all six scenes into one palette.

## Finish and texture

Native C01/D01 show clean filled boundaries and selective internal planes. R01 has visibly granular material behind cleaner root/rock shapes. D02 uses glow and suppressed interior detail. Use gradients/glow to explain light, energy or depth; keep contour and material information readable without them. Our approved Mira texture has a softer painted grain than C01's cleaner fills. That difference is visible in the [face comparison](../analysis/visual-comparisons/character-construction-comparison.jpg) and remains unresolved as a general new-character construction capability.

## Exceptions and project decisions

Source typography includes chapter headings, diagram labels, interfaces, speech and end cards. Source live-action promotional sections have real lighting, cameras and texture. These are contextual examples, not permission to add decoration to our film. The user specifically requested **no on-screen title**; all four original studies obey that. Use text only when a future story requires it and the user has not prohibited it. No source logo, character, scene tracing or soundtrack has been repurposed as an original asset.

The production architecture is a hybrid: preserve approved layered character art, deform a connected skin within its tested pose envelope, draw explanatory props as editable procedural contours, and author expressions/alternate views as needed. See [implementation](ANIMATION_IMPLEMENTATION.md). A beautiful still is an asset approval; a new action, view or scene needs its own rendered review.
