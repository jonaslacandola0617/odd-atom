# Evidence index and scope

This is version 1 of an evidence-backed production foundation. All five archive videos have been inventoried and all **236,097 frames** measured. All **46 first/middle/last storyboard pages** have been visually navigated. **355 conservative proposals are not verified shots.** Nineteen selected cases below have substantive annotations: sixteen temporal windows and three construction stills. Neither this subset nor the new tests establishes archive-wide animation mastery.

Frame ranges are zero-based, half-open `[start,end)`. Source PTS is recorded in each JSON, not inferred from the container duration. All five actual sources happen to be 60 fps. Complete hashes, codec, audio streams and picture endpoints are in [manifest.json](../references/manifest.json). Short names in the guides resolve here.

Every case links its exact evidence manifest, consecutive contact sheets and original source hash. Dense PNGs are regenerable and ignored; selected native design frames and crops are retained. Source excerpts are **reference clips**, never our original demonstration videos. Some exported investigation names are misleading: `tree-conduit-flow` proved to be a radial-growth diagram; `room-object-change` proved to be a hold. The annotations preserve the correction.

Measurements: [creator core/hand](../analysis/motion-tracks/reference_fd02030ee890/creator-core-and-hand.json), [eye mask](../analysis/motion-tracks/reference_fd02030ee890/creator-eye-mask.json), [face geometry](../analysis/composition-diagrams/creator-face-geometry.json), [forest registration](../analysis/motion-tracks/reference_46db30e386c9/forest-registered-layer-comparison.json), [boat hull mask](../analysis/motion-tracks/reference_9a02fcef71d3/boat-lime-hull-mask.json), [local palettes](../analysis/visual-comparisons/frame-local-palettes.json). Mask areas/centroids and image registration are measurements of pixels, not recovered rig controls.

The offline transcripts cover complete soundtracks in 25-second chunks, but remain **unverified ASR** with spelling/word errors and coarse timing. No direct listening review, music/SFX isolation or exact word sync is claimed. [Remaining work](../remaining-work.json) records the outstanding full-shot semantic, locomotion, across-angle and perceptual audio studies.
