Evidence: [C01, F01, D01–D03, L01, R01, A02, A04, A06](EVIDENCE_INDEX.md). Coordinates below are approximate analyst layout descriptions where stated. They are not recovered source layout constraints.

## Select a visual task before placing objects

In C01 the chest-level light and centered face form one focal cluster. Peripheral panels frame that cluster; their reduced prominence reinforces loss. The viewer does not need to chase a second equally bright event. D01 uses a left carrier and right bending worker with a crop patch between/behind them: relative pose and prop placement convey two kinds of labor. In L01 the diagram owns the center and the background becomes plain, because identifying layers is the task.

F01 makes the foreground an occluder rather than a competing subject. The warm opening/path remains a destination while dark branches move outward. A04 uses a top-down course whose stable wall and right-side racers make the green boat's wrong turn apparent. Motion is understandable because there is a frame of reference. A06 retains a color-distinct minority row as the population expands, giving a comparison anchor through the scale change.

## How to stage a new explanation

Write an attention order using stable object IDs. Establish the necessary landmarks before the action. Choose one initial comparison axis (left/right, before/after, inside/outside or small/large). Reserve space for the consequence before placing decorative scenery. Keep entering information near the current eye path when continuity helps; use a deliberate reframe when a new scale is necessary. These are production choices supported by the examples, not a measured rule-of-thirds law.

Animate the composition when the information changes. A02 keeps room shell/person stable while belongings vanish, so the viewer can compare the result directly. A06 changes the field's scale and density to express population. C turns a small water carrier into the region that reveals the next idea; incoming imagery is aligned and zoomed so it does not appear as a miniature rectangular slide.

## Readability audit

Inspect at normal viewing size as well as native pixels. Can the subject silhouette be named immediately? Can the viewer locate the cause, contact and result? Does a foreground layer hide an important interaction? Are scale changes explained by context or transition? Does texture survive as material without becoming competing contrast? Compare against the relevant source family; a full dark technical field is not an appropriate density target for a sunlit harvest scene.
