## Visual family and meaningful differences

Botanical history and tissue mechanics; rich landscapes alternate with spare diagrams.

Natural scenes use depth, varied silhouettes and warm openings; cell diagrams reduce scenery and repeat readable structures. Rapid live-action testimonial cuts are not animation timing templates.

**Medium:** clean filled, vector-like illustrated shapes with selective planes, texture and glow. The delivered movie is flattened; original authoring software/rig is not proven.

**Characters:** Birds, occasional scale humans and contextual animals; many shots have no human. Plants/cells are explanatory subjects rather than all anthropomorphic characters. Unique identities/counts remain unverified.

**Environments/objects:** Layered evergreen/lush/desert/snow forests, streams and canopy views; violet cell/tube/cross-section diagrams; city/tree metaphor; live-action restoration/product sections.
