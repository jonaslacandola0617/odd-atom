## Additional thematic selection

These are candidate episode titles/topics for a future verified search, not newly retrieved sources or completed studies. Their current titles, links, and availability have not been checked in this session:

- **Optimistic Nihilism:** philosophy, meaning, reassurance, and whether the imagery makes an abstract argument accessible.
- **The Egg:** fiction and identity; distinguish narrative storytelling from evidence-based explanation.
- **An Antidote to Dissatisfaction:** gratitude and habits; examine ordinary life, personal framing, and practical suggestions.
- **Universal basic income:** economics and policy; examine tradeoffs, institutions, and uncertainty without reducing the subject to one character's choices.
- **The war on drugs:** public policy and unintended consequences; examine how consequences and alternatives are compared.
- **Time, mortality, and the shape of a life:** examine emotional pacing and relationships rather than catastrophe spectacle.
- **Automation and work:** examine workplaces, incentives, and the relationship between individuals and systems.
- **Consumer decisions and ethical tradeoffs:** examine food, waste, resources, and where personal choices meet structural constraints.

This adds philosophy, fiction, psychology, economics, policy, mortality, work, and ethics to the palette. It is a coverage plan, not a claim that the topics' scientific or social arguments have been researched for our scripts.

## What to capture when playback works

For each selected episode, record timestamped evidence of the opening promise, the first concrete example, a human acting passage, a mechanism/system explanation, a qualification or counterpoint, a major transition, and the resolution. Sample enough adjacent frames to distinguish object deformation from camera motion. Review audio over the same passages before making claims about voice, music, or effects.

Compare each passage's purpose and how long it needs, rather than treating a high cut rate or constant movement as the source of quality. Keep observed details, inferred meaning, and proposed original treatments in separate notes.
