# Final singularity audio and reusable music

Committed effects, atmosphere, music and final-mix M4A stems. `music-cues/` contains nine newly composed original FLAC cues and their catalog for future reuse. Material SFX are reused from the imported Curio Bureau library. The original Knox source/timing is unchanged. Rebuild with `npm run sound:singularity-v4`; mood timing, placement and measurable mix priorities are in `docs/production/singularity/final-mix/sound-design.json`. No voice or generative-service request is made. Direct audio audition is unavailable here.
